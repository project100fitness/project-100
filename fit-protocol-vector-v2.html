V2 REFUEL

During the training session (States A and C)

During the training session (09:30 morning reference); moves with the evening workout in State C.

600-750 mL cold filtered water

  • Perfect Sports BCAA HyperClear (1 scoop / 7.5 g BCAAs 2:1:1)
  • RapiDrem Electrolyte Powder (1 scoop, about 6.5 g)
  • Kirkland Himalayan Pink Salt 1/16 tsp
  • 2-3 Medjool dates chewed solid, separately
Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
3,750 mg leucine-led BCAAs, 1,000 mg potassium, 100 mg magnesium, about 36-54 g fast carbohydrate from dates.
Stack interaction
Magnesium 341-646 mg + high- dose vitamin C + continuous bile trickle — Loose stools are predictable system output; log and moderate fat bolus before blaming single foods.
Margin
Supplemental magnesium: training baseline 341 mg; up to 646 mg with options, versus 350 mg/day supplemental UL. Rest totals are lower and not fully quantified.
Monitor
Stool, pain, weakness, sleep and performance noted; options marked as taken or skipped.
Stop rule
Recurrent watery stools with weight loss, oily stools or significant abdominal pain: stop and seek assessment.

V2 REFUEL

Ingredient facts

  • BCAA Hyper Clear (7.5g): Branched-chain amino acids during training. Provides leucine, isoleucine, valine to support muscle protein synthesis and reduce breakdown during the session.
  • Electrolytes (1000mg K, 100mg Mg): Potassium, magnesium, calcium, and vitamin C. Replaces what's lost in sweat. Critical for muscle function and cramp prevention during training.
  • Medjool Dates (2-3): Fast-acting natural carbs chewed solid. Provides quick glucose for training energy without gut distress. Whole food > processed sugars.

Training days only, 09:30 in-session. Whole-food carb source paired with aminos and electrolytes.
