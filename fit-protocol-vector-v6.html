🌙 V6 EVENING — Key Actives (STIM-FREE)

  • 8gCitrulline
  • 3.2gBeta-Alanine
  • 5gBetaine total
  • 1gCreatine
  • 4-5gTaurine total
  • 0mgCaffeine
  • Creatine (1g native): From the Pump Xtreme tub. Supports ATP regeneration for strength and power. Evening dose is part of the daily saturation strategy — timing matters less than consistency.
  • Citrulline (8g) + Beta-Alanine (3.2g): Same pump/endurance base as V1's Igniter — full clinical doses for nitric oxide and muscular endurance. The stim-free formula keeps the performance actives without caffeine.
  • Betaine (5g total): 2.5g native from Pump tub + 2.5g standalone NOW Sports powder. Double the standard dose — supports power output, cellular hydration, and methylation. V6-day total reaches 5000mg.
  • Taurine (4-5g total): 2g native from Pump + 2-3g added. Cell volumizer and osmolyte for sarcoplasmic swelling. Supports hydration without stimulant load — no sleep interference.
  • Creatine (1g native): From the Pump tub. Supports ATP regeneration. Part of daily saturation strategy — timing matters less than consistency. State C day total: 2.5g with branch arithmetic.
  • L-Arginine (500mg): Nitric oxide precursor (corrected from agmatine per label audit). Supports blood flow without stimulants.
  • L-Tyrosine (500mg) + Choline (300mg): Focus and neurotransmitter support without caffeine. Tyrosine is a dopamine precursor; choline supports acetylcholine.
  • Huperzine-A (50mcg): Lower evening dose vs V1's 200mcg morning. Extends acetylcholine activity with a 10-24h tail — consider sleep impact.

STIM-FREE: 0mg caffeine verified. Designed for evening training without disrupting sleep. Unused since May 2026 (training schedule) — not deleted from protocol.
