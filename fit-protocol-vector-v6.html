V6 PUMP

V6 · EVENING OVERRIDE

V6 PUMP

Evening training only (State C); replaces V1

  • Evening training override; 20–30 minutes before the evening session.
  • Citicoline is a separate capsule at T−30 minutes.
  • Never combined with V1 on the same calendar day.

500–600 mL cold filtered water

  • ALLMAX Impact PUMP Xtreme (Blue Raspberry): 2 scoops / 31 g
  • ALLMAX MuscleEAA Xtreme (Cherry Limeade): 1 scoop / 17.73 g
  • Compound Solutions / ALLMAX PeakO2: 2,000 mg by mass
  • NOW Betaine Anhydrous (TMG): 2,500 mg
  • ALLMAX Essentials Citrulline Malate 2:1: 4–6 g
  • ALLMAX Essentials Pure Taurine Powder: 2–3 g
  • AOR Xerenoos Citicoline: 1 capsule / 250 mg, swallowed separately with water

Protocol

Purpose: Evening pre-workout preparation without added caffeine in the recorded Pump formula.

Composition:

  • ALLMAX PUMP and MuscleEAA, plus PeakO2, betaine, citrulline malate and taurine.
  • EAAs supply essential amino acids; citrulline participates in nitric-oxide production, and betaine contributes to cellular water balance.

Usage:

  • 20–30 minutes before evening training, in 500–600 mL cold water.
  • Citicoline stays separate at T−30. V6 replaces V1; never use both on the same day.
V6 source notes & deployment rules
Recorded branch
State C: evening training override, documented 5 October 2026.
Exclusion
V1 and V6 are never used on the same calendar day. Both are inactive on rest days.
Shaker exclusions
No added pink salt. Native creatine monohydrate 1000 mg and native sodium 220 mg (Canadian formula); stimulant-free PUMP base.
Companion routing
V2 remains intra-session; V4 moves after the evening workout; V5 is inactive; V10 remains the night anchor.

V6 PUMP

Ingredient facts

  • Creatine (1g native): From the Pump Xtreme tub. Supports ATP regeneration for strength and power. Evening dose is part of the daily saturation strategy — timing matters less than consistency.
  • Citrulline (8g) + Beta-Alanine (3.2g): Same pump/endurance base as V1's Igniter — full clinical doses for nitric oxide and muscular endurance. The stim-free formula keeps the performance actives without caffeine.
  • Betaine (5g total): 2.5g native from Pump tub + 2.5g standalone NOW Sports powder. Double the standard dose — supports power output, cellular hydration, and methylation. V6-day total reaches 5000mg.
  • Taurine (4-5g total): 2g native from Pump + 2-3g added. Cell volumizer and osmolyte for sarcoplasmic swelling. Supports hydration without stimulant load — no sleep interference.
  • Creatine (1g native): From the Pump tub. Supports ATP regeneration. Part of daily saturation strategy — timing matters less than consistency. State C formula-native monohydrate: 1,000 mg, counted separately from standalone HCl 1,500 mg/day.
  • L-Arginine (500mg): Nitric oxide precursor (corrected from agmatine per label audit). Supports blood flow without stimulants.
  • L-Tyrosine (500mg) + Choline (300mg): Focus and neurotransmitter support without caffeine. Tyrosine is a dopamine precursor; choline supports acetylcholine.
  • Huperzine-A (50mcg): Lower evening dose vs V1's 200mcg morning. Extends acetylcholine activity with a 10-24h tail — consider sleep impact.

STIM-FREE: 0mg caffeine verified. Designed for evening training without disrupting sleep. Unused since May 2026 (training schedule) — not deleted from protocol.
