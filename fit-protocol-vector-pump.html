Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
Preserved strictly for structural reference. Non-stimulant evening alternative that must never be co- administered with a morning stimulant session.
Stack interaction
Citicoline 250 mg + Alpha-GPC 200 mg + choline salts + DMAE + multivitamin choline 57 mg, against Huperzine A 200 mcg — Excess cholinergic tone: bradycardia, fasciculations, salivation, GI cramps that can mimic overtraining.
Margin
Inactive: never used with V1.
Monitor
Branch selected correctly; morning stimulant session recorded.
Stop rule
Do not combine this inactive evening alternative with V1.
