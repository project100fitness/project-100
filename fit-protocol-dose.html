Before I change anything

  • Is the measurement comparable? Same device, same conditions?
  • Did sleep, digestion or load change first?
  • Am I changing one variable, or three at once?

What conditional means in my book

  • Calcium capsule removed from the current record; no calcium capsule in any grid.
  • Liver-GI Detox and Milk Thistle — only in their support windows.
  • Medi-C and Hey Relax in V10 — titrated, never standing nightly doses.

Staying safe

Four combinations I watch

Piperine and clearance

14.5 mg on training days can raise exposure to medicines and to the stack itself. I disclose it to prescribers.

Cholinergic stack

  • Citicoline and Alpha-GPC together can cause cramps, salivation, twitching or slow pulse — symptoms I treat as a stop signal, not overtraining.
  • My cholinergic inputs are citicoline 250 mg (my only capsule) plus formula-native DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg inside the V1 Igniter dose — or Huperzine-A 50 mcg inside the V6 Pump dose — plus Alpha-GPC inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day: 500 mg inside formulas).

Never standalone pills: I never bought or added them. See Chapter 22.

R-ALA and low fuel

300 mg R-ALA with only 19 g carbohydrate in V4 means post-training sweating, tremor or confusion gets glucose awareness first.

Magnesium, vitamin C and bile

The osmotic chain plus continuous bile trickle makes loose stools predictable system output. I log it and adjust fat load before blaming one food.

Staying safe

My red flags

  • Progressive weakness, new numbness, bowel or bladder change.
  • Jaundice, fever with abdominal pain, recurrent watery stools with weight loss.
  • Palpitations, fainting, unusually slow pulse, recurrent hypoglycaemia-type symptoms.
  • Sleep disruption that does not settle when caffeine moves earlier.

I never treat a combination symptom by adding its opposite from a nutrient product shelf.

Safety cascades, expanded

Five cascades

Cascade 1 — Stimulant

  • V1 (~373 mg caffeine) is the only high-stimulant vector; tea (~40-70 mg) is timed two hours earlier so the training window is read cleanly.
  • V6 exists so evening training does not borrow against sleep.

A late stimulant is never the fix for a short night.

Cascade 2 — Cholinergic (corrected and locked)

Inputs:

  • citicoline 250 mg (sole capsule) + formula-native DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg (V1) or Huperzine-A 50 mcg (V6) + Alpha-GPC inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day 500 mg) + counted multivitamin choline contribution.
  • The inhibitor IS in the stack — which is why the firewall (V1/V6: at most one on training days; neither on rest days) matters: the two Huperzine-A doses never stack same-day, and its ~10-24 h half-life is the reason the cap is architectural, not voluntary.

S016 is not my protocol.

Cascade 3 — Piperine

  • BioPerine 5 mg with curcumin 475 mg slows glucuronidation clearance.
  • That is the intended mechanism for curcumin exposure and the watched mechanism for anything else cleared the same way — one line, both readings, always together.

Cascade 4 — R-ALA / glucose

R-ALA 300 mg (swallowed whole) before a 19 g carbohydrate reload: symptoms after training are triaged as glucose first. The capsule never enters liquid.

Cascade 5 — Osmotic GI (post-cholecystectomy)

  • Betaine, taurine, magnesium and related osmolytes share a gut without a bile reservoir; smaller fat portions, slower eating and meal enzymes are the pacing answer.
  • Historical steatosis keeps liver items (Liver-GI 50/50/47.5 mg class, conditional milk thistle) monitored rather than automatic.

Canon card

The locked positions: one betaine, one cholinergic capsule, and V1/V6: at most one on training days; neither on rest days.

Eight locked rules

BetaineNOW Sports Betaine Powder — 2,500 mg in V1 and in V6; the only betaine item. No other betaine brand is part of this protocol.
