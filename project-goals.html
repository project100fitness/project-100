Progression →

Quarterly reviews and symptom-guided progression. No fixed growth rate and no completion deadline.

Three phases

Non-Negotiable

LOAD FOLLOWS TOLERANCE. A rigid +2.5 lb every three weeks is not a current V2.0 prescription. If pain changes form or neurological signs appear, stop and assess.

  1. I Months 1–3

    Rebuild the routine

    Regular training, sleep and repeatable setups come first.

    Historical planning phase · Rebuild the routine

  2. II Months 4–8

    Repeat the loading signal

    Supported work and recovery guide progression; no automatic load escalation.

    Historical planning phase · Repeat the loading signal

  3. III Months 9–12

    Review and adjust

    Compare body composition, waist and function under consistent conditions.
