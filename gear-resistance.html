The Physics Behind It

The manifest lists a 360 lb KUZARO set and a 330 lb HPYGN set. These are combined nominal ratings; band tension changes with the tubes selected and their stretch. The kit collection allows different resistance combinations.

Band systems & resistance grades

    6 BUNDLES · 4 KITS

    Purpose: Six configurations cover presses, pulls, isolation and unilateral curls.

    Usage: Record the trainer-selected setup; bundle labels identify equipment, not prescribed force.

    Six named matched-band configurations form the KUZARO setup. Bundle ratings describe the selected tubes; resistance changes with stretch.

    Bundle connections & protective tape

    Black Renfrew cloth tape at the joint loops, replacing rigid plastic ties. Connections vary by setup. Historical connector counts are not working-load ratings; use the trainer-confirmed configuration.

  • HPYGN Heavy-Duty Compression System

    330 LB SET

    Purpose: Shrouded bands provide a portable resistance option.

    Usage: Use the recorded low-anchor setup and control the return through each repetition.

    Design & specifications

    Gray-black multi-layered bands with black nylon shrouds and carabiner clips. Owner-confirmed short-band configuration: two 65 lb bands per side, paired twice (four bands total), shortened at the low Iron Tower anchor and routed into the Tribe bar’s rotating eyelets.

    Nominal ratings total 130 lb per side and 260 lb combined; resistance changes with stretch.

Equipment identities and listed ratings follow the supplied PROJECT 100 visual recognition database. Tube ratings, combined set ratings and force at a particular stretch are different measurements.
