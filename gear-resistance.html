The Physics Behind It

The manifest lists a 360 lb KUZARO set and a 330 lb HPYGN set. These are combined nominal ratings; band tension changes with the tubes selected and their stretch. The kit collection allows different resistance combinations.

Band systems & resistance grades

  • KUZARO · Multi-band setup

    6 BUNDLES · 4 KITS

    Six named matched-band configurations form the KUZARO setup. Bundle ratings describe the selected tubes; resistance changes with stretch.

    Hex-Grid bundle directory
    Juggernaut255 lb

    3 × 85 lb · matched bundle · Solid black

    Chest · Chest presses, standing presses and horizontal pushing.

    Titan195 lb

    3 × 65 lb · matched bundle · Dark gray

    Back · Rows, lat pull-downs and posterior-chain work.

    Enforcer165 lb

    3 × 55 lb · matched bundle · Medium gray

    Triceps · Overhand/underhand extensions, press-downs and lockout work.

    Viper135 lb

    3 × 45 lb · matched bundle · Medium-light gray

    Reserve / drop sets · Shoulder raises, drop sets and finisher work.

    Scalpel105 lb

    3 × 35 lb · matched bundle · Light gray

    Delts / isolation · Deltoid flys, precise isolation and warm-up sets.

    Twin Cobras75 lb / hand

    2 × 75 lb · separate bands · Very dark gray / soft black

    Biceps / unilateral · Two separate bands for unilateral curls, one band per hand with individual factory clips.

    Individual tube grades · 360 lb listed set

    • 85 lb · 38.5 kg Solid black
    Bundle connections & protective tape

    Black Renfrew cloth tape at the joint loops, replacing rigid plastic ties. Eight JRSGS carabiners in the documented bundle setup. Twin Cobras use individual factory clips.

  • HPYGN Heavy-Duty Compression System

    330 LB SET

    Gray-black multi-layered bands with black nylon shrouds and carabiner clips. Recorded short-band configuration: two 65 lb bands, doubled and shortened at the low Iron Tower anchor, routed into the Tribe bar’s rotating eyelets. Their nominal ratings total 130 lb; resistance changes with stretch.

Equipment identities and listed ratings follow the supplied PROJECT 100 visual recognition database. Tube ratings, combined set ratings and force at a particular stretch are different measurements.
