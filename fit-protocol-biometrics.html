Scan-day controls

The morning

Fasted, on waking, post-void, before training, before fluids. Same InBody unit at Gym Fit Forme as the 14 Dec 2025 benchmark.

The 24 hours before

At least a 24-hour buffer after heavy lifting. No sauna, no alcohol in the prior 24 hours. Normal dinner, normal hydration the night before.

What I will report

SMM, total weight and body fat from that single visit, with time, last meal, last workout and sleep logged beside the printout.

The window

14–21 December 2026 — twelve months after the benchmark, under the same controls. Whether to deload the week before stays my open decision.

BIA reads water (Chapter 14): creatine, betaine, taurine, glycogen and potassium can move a printout 2-4 lb with no new contractile protein. Controls do not remove that — they make it repeatable, so a change means tissue, not weather.

  • 01OpenPhysiology, measurement, training, nutrition and laboratoryFive readings of the same arc+
Five lenses on the measured arc

Physiology, measurement, training, nutrition and laboratory

Lens 1 — Physiology. At 170 cm and age 46, my reconciled ceiling (FFM 162-164 lb at 14.5%, FFMI 25.71) yields 91.5-93.0 lb true SMM at a 56-57% SMM/FFM partition. The 90-93 lb corridor is therefore a tissue claim, not a scale claim. BIA adds 2-4 lb of osmolyte-and glycogen-driven intracellular water (creatine HCl 1.5 g/day, betaine 2,500 mg, taurine 3-4 g, potassium 1,000 mg/scoop) — the 92-97 lb printout corridor is the same tissue read wet.

Lens 2 — Measurement. BIA is a conductivity estimate. My controls (fasted, waking, post-void, same unit, 24 h buffer) shrink pre-analytical variance so that quarter-to-quarter deltas mean tissue, not hydration theatre. The unverified Apr 2026 scan is the worked example: 93.7 lb at 30.2% body fat is internally inconsistent with the Dec-to-Aug trajectory and stays excluded until re-measured.

Lens 3 — Training. The telemetry justifies supported, repeatable loading over heroic sessions: connective tissue remodels at roughly one-third muscle speed at 46, so the hex-grid and chest-supported work (Ch. 6) are the delivery system for the corridor, not an accessory to it.

Lens 4 — Nutrition & vectors. The same osmolytes that inflate BIA are functional in the vectors (Ch. 8, 20, 21): betaine as methyl donor/osmolyte, taurine for bile conjugation in a post-cholecystectomy gut, creatine in two 750 mg pulses outside the pump shakers. Measurement honesty and formulation intent are read together — never separately.

Lens 5 — Laboratory integrity. Biotin 425 mcg is disclosed, not washed out; testosterone verdicts require morning fasting draws (the 13:28, 7.4 nmol/L afternoon value is my cited non-diagnostic example). A scan and a lab value earn belief the same way: declared conditions, comparable repeats.

The road ahead

Next scan: 14–21 December 2026

When and where

14–21 December 2026 — Gym Fit Forme, same InBody unit as the 14 Dec 2025 benchmark.

Conditions

Fasted, on waking, post-void, before training and before fluids. Normal hydration. At least a 24-hour buffer after heavy sessions; no sauna or alcohol in the prior 24 hours.

What I will report

SMM, total weight and body fat from that single visit — with time, last meal and last workout noted.

Open decision

Whether to run a standardized deload week before the scan so swelling does not blur the baseline.

How I will judge the result

  • 90-93 lb true tissue is success in the validated corridor.
  • 92-97 lb on the printout under full hydration is the expected BIA range for that tissue.
  • Weekly logs (performance, sleep, digestion, waist, photos) matter more than one noisy reading.
