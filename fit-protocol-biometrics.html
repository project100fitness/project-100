Measuring honestly

The FFMI check on my own numbers

  • Bioelectrical impedance does not weigh dry muscle.
  • It estimates SMM from body water, so creatine, electrolytes, glycogen and a large drink can move the printout by 2 to 4 lb without any new contractile tissue.

Folder 04

Five lenses on the measured arc

The dated ranges are estimates, not validated measurements of dry muscle.

Physiology, measurement, training, nutrition and laboratory

Lens 1 — Historical model.

  • At 170 cm and age 46, the old calculation used FFM 162-164 lb, FFMI 25.71 and an assumed 56-57% SMM/FFM partition.
  • Its 91.5-93.0 lb result is an illustrative estimate, not measured tissue or a validated ceiling.
  • The current correction register supersedes the old tissue claim and fixed 2-4 lb water conversion.
  • The 90-93 lb and 92-97 lb bands are dated estimates.
  • BIA does not isolate dry contractile tissue; the updated edition supplies no new body-composition measurement.

Lens 2 — Measurement.

  • BIA is a conductivity estimate.
  • My controls (fasted, waking, post-void, same unit, 24 h buffer) shrink pre-analytical variance so that quarter-to-quarter deltas mean tissue, not hydration theatre.
  • The unverified Apr 2026 scan is the worked example: 93.7 lb at 30.2% body fat is internally inconsistent with the Dec-to-Aug trajectory and stays excluded until re-measured.

Lens 3 — Training.

  • The telemetry justifies supported, repeatable loading over heroic sessions: connective tissue remodels at roughly one-third muscle speed at 46, so the hex-grid and chest-supported work (Ch. 6) are the delivery system for the corridor, not an accessory to it.

Lens 4 — Nutrition & vectors.

  • The same osmolytes that inflate BIA are functional in the vectors (Ch. 8, 20, 21): betaine as methyl donor/osmolyte, taurine for bile conjugation in a post-cholecystectomy gut, creatine in two 750 mg pulses outside the pump shakers.

Measurement honesty and formulation intent are read together — never separately.

Lens 5 — Laboratory integrity.

  • Biotin 425 mcg is disclosed, not washed out; testosterone verdicts require morning fasting draws (the 13:28, 7.4 nmol/L afternoon value is my cited non-diagnostic example).
  • A scan and a lab value earn belief the same way: declared conditions, comparable repeats.
Folder 05

The road ahead

My next scan is already a controlled experiment.

When and where

14–21 December 2026 — Gym Fit Forme, same InBody unit as the 2 Dec 2025 benchmark.

Conditions
  • Fasted, on waking, post-void, before training and before fluids.
  • Normal hydration.
  • At least a 24-hour buffer after heavy sessions; no sauna or alcohol in the prior 24 hours.
What I will report

SMM, total weight and body fat from that single visit — with time, last meal and last workout noted.

Open decision

Whether to run a standardized deload week before the scan so swelling does not blur the baseline.

How I will judge the result

  • 90-93 lb is a dated 2026 planning estimate, not a validated dry-tissue corridor.
  • 92-97 lb is a separate historical BIA estimate. Compare actual scans under consistent conditions; do not subtract a fixed water allowance.
  • Weekly logs (performance, sleep, digestion, waist, photos) matter more than one noisy reading.
