Open PHONE for short cards. IMAGES contains 128 complete reference panels; these are intentionally tall so no data is shrunk. PAGES contains the editable layouts; open them locally with their adjacent ASSETS and FONTS folders intact.

All information text is at least 42 px on the 720 px export: 18.7 px at 320 px display width, 22.8 px at 390 px. Amounts are 48 px and headings 58 px. Large clear Inter text, high contrast, left alignment and generous line spacing. Screen diagonals alone do not establish reading size; sizing is checked by the available display width.

Pill symbols correspond to the stated number in each product row. Dashed symbols are conditional or intermittent. Capsule and softgel drawings are distinguished. Counts refer to the recorded personal routine, not a recommendation.

Illustrations retain the source family and section grouping, reflowed into individual reading panels. Anatomy captions explain basic functions, not a demonstrated benefit of adjacent products. Decorative pills from the illustration layers are excluded from finished panels. Source artwork files are working layers, not completed diagrams.
