Vectors V1–V10 — Analysis

A vector is a timed delivery bundle: several ingredients aimed at one outcome in one window (Encyclopedia Part III). Training branch: V1, V2, V3, V4, V10. Rest branch: V3, V5, V10. V6 PUMP is the evening-training override and is inactive in States A and B. Vectors are powerful because they stack — and every important risk in this book concentrates in the five cascades of Encyclopedia Part IV: piperine, cholinergic, ALA–insulin autoimmune, osmotic, and stimulant.
