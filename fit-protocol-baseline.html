Introduction - The architecture of return

PROJECT 100 records a personal return to training and a long-term ambition: 100 lb of skeletal muscle mass with body fat below 15 percent. Those numbers express the author's goal. They do not establish a

medical threshold, a guaranteed outcome or a deadline.

A difficult personal period disrupted routines. In 2026, attention returned to health and gym training. The current source profile reports age 46 and body weight

90.76 kg. The February publications recorded an earlier baseline of age 45, weight 197.1 lb, skeletal muscle

mass 88.2 lb and body fat 22.9 percent. These are separate dated records. This edition does not

manufacture a single exact chronology from conflicting earlier dates.

The author reports improvements in recovery, digestion and injury symptoms since rebuilding the routine.

Those experiences belong in the journey. They cannot isolate the contribution of each supplement or prove

that a large combined stack is harmless. The edition therefore gives every item a recorded dose, use status and evidence limit. Brand changes and label versions matter as much as product names.

The current gym branch uses morning workouts. Vectors V3, V1, V2, V4 and V10 describe that branch when

consumed. The rest branch uses V3, V5 and V10 when consumed, with a smaller pill routine. V6 PUMP is the evening-training override, excluded from morning training and rest days. Zinc Supreme is removed, separate copper is paused, and boron is one 3 mg

capsule. Optional half-dose Medi-C and rare Hey Relax use remain optional. This publication does not turn

them into daily requirements.

Clinical analysis - rebuilding after a prolonged interruption.

A prolonged interruption can affect strength, nutrition, sleep and recovery. Retraining builds capacity through repeatable mechanical loading, adequate energy and sleep. The 2026 ACSM position supports individualized

resistance training and regular participation; it does not validate any fixed excavation duration or load

percentage. Progress is judged against the author's own comparable measurements, function and

health.

Chapter 1 - The prime directive - what 100 means

The target is 100 lb of reported skeletal muscle mass, not lean body mass or total body weight.

Bioimpedance analysis (BIA) estimates skeletal muscle mass (SMM) from an algorithm sensitive to

intracellular and extracellular water, glycogen and electrolyte shifts. It does not directly weigh muscle tissue.

Under multi-gram osmotic supplementation, fluid compartment changes can move the estimate without any change in contractile protein. Use the same device and repeat comparable measurements - waking, post-

void, pre-intake, similar hydration and meal state - before interpreting a change as tissue growth. Track body

weight, waist, performance and symptoms alongside the estimate.

Body fat below 15 percent remains a personal aspiration, not a universal requirement for insulin sensitivity, hormone health or successful training. The historical book used competitive bodybuilding as a visual

reference. A competition division cannot establish an individual's biological ceiling. The IFBB distinguishes

Men's 212 Bodybuilding from Classic Physique; height and weight tables from Classic Physique must not be transferred to the 212 division. Retain the reference as inspiration only.

Analysis 1A - BIA physics and the 212 reference. Multifrequency BIA models total body water, then

derives fat-free mass and SMM by regression. Creatine HCl (1.5 g daily), high-dose electrolytes and carbohydrate loading all expand intracellular water and will inflate SMM readings. This is why the

chronometer standardizes measurement timing and why quarterly review beats weekly reaction. The

prime directive phrase "Train Like Two. Eat for Two" is retained as historical branding describing ambition and sustained effort. It does not prescribe twice the workload or twice the calories.

The Prime Directive and the Return

PRIME DIRECTIVE

Rebuild to 100 lb skeletal muscle mass at under 15% body fat, without trading long-term health for short- term numbers. Every protocol in this book must pass three locks: it must support training, it must be tolerable day after day, and it must leave a clean monitoring trail. If a protocol fails any lock, the protocol changes — not the target.

How the target is measured (Encyclopedia Ch. 1): 100 lb is a bioimpedance-estimated skeletal muscle mass, tracked on the same device under standardised conditions — waking, post-void, pre- intake, similar hydration and meal state. It is a personal target, not a clinical threshold. Creatine, electrolytes, carbohydrate and fluid shifts can move the estimate without new muscle, so quarterly review beats weekly reaction.
