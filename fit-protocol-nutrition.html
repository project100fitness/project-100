Recovery salad ingredients & portion rules

Logged ingredients include romaine, kale, mixed beans, lupini, cucumber, pepper, pickled beets, olives, tuna, feta and eggs; some bowls include tomato, cabbage, spinach or grilled chicken.

Mixed beans + lupini + eggs: two eggs. Eggs without beans: four eggs. Heavy dressing: avocado, 2–3 tbsp [30–45 mL] avocado oil and white vinegar. Light dressing: extra virgin olive oil and balsamic glaze.

The bowls use the same large 15-inch mixer bowl. Prepared dressing can remain at the bottom, so prepared totals and consumed totals differ.

Guidebook V3.7 · harvested records · Encyclopedia V3.6 · complete record

Analysis 5A - GI physiology without a gallbladder. Continuous bile trickle favours smaller, more frequent fat loads over single large boluses.

Magnesium from products (341 mg baseline, up to 446-646 mg with Medi-C or Hey Relax) exceeds the 350 mg upper level for products and medicines and, combined with high-dose vitamin C, creates an osmotic chain that compounds bile-related looseness.

Stool changes are therefore logged as system data, not personal failure.

Nutrition — Analysis

MECHANISM

  • Protein drives muscle protein synthesis
  • Total energy decides whether that synthesis can run
  • Meal timing decides tolerance
  • Your system holds protein constant across training and rest days and moves energy with output — the reverse (cutting protein on rest days) is the most common comeback error, because repair peaks when you are not training.

STACK INTERACTION — THE GUT IS THE BOTTLENECK

Hydration: electrolytes accompany training water, not as an afterthought; cramps are logged as data, not endured as noise.
