Daily anchor; optional when consumed on rest days

04:00; training and rest

Cold filtered water; shaker volume unspecified

  • MuscleMeds Carnivor Beef Protein Isolate (33.9 g Canadian scoop)
  • PROMISE Pure Marine Collagen Peptan 10 g
  • Rootalive Organic Gelatinized Black Maca 5 g
  • ALLMAX Liquid L- Carnitine 1500 (15 mL / 1,500 mg)
  • MegaFood C Defense Gummy (1, chewed)
  • NAKA Platinum Vital Greens (20 mL separate flush)

Protocol

Purpose: Morning protein anchor to help meet daily protein needs and supply amino acids for tissue maintenance and recovery.

Composition:

  • Beef isolate is the main protein source; marine collagen adds complementary peptides.
  • Maca adds a plant-based component; L-carnitine participates in fatty-acid transport for energy metabolism.

Usage:

  • 04:00 on the recorded daily schedule; optional when consumed on rest days.
  • Mix with cold water.
  • Chew the gummy and take the greens separately.
Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
23 g beef protein, 10 g marine collagen, 1.5 g free carnitine, whole-food ascorbate. Canadian Carnivor panel: zero added creatine. Greens taken as separate 20 mL flush.
Stack interaction
Magnesium 341-646 mg + high- dose vitamin C + continuous bile trickle — Loose stools are predictable system output; log and moderate fat bolus before blaming single foods.
Margin
Magnesium from products: training baseline 341 mg; up to 646 mg with options, versus 350 mg/day UL for products and medicines. Rest totals are lower and not fully quantified.
Monitor
Stool, pain, weakness, sleep and performance noted; options marked as taken or skipped.
Stop rule
Recurrent watery stools with weight loss, oily stools or significant abdominal pain: stop and seek assessment.

Ingredient facts

  • Beef Protein Isolate (33.9g): Carnivor beef protein — dairy-free alternative to whey. Fast-absorbing post-wake protein to break the overnight fast and supply aminos.
  • Marine Collagen (10g AM): PROMISE uses fish-derived Peptan type I collagen peptides. Hydrolysis produces smaller peptides supplying glycine, proline and hydroxyproline for connective-tissue turnover in skin, tendons and bone. Marine describes the source, not a universal advantage over bovine; this matrix complements the main muscle-protein serving.
  • Black Maca (5g): Gelatinized black maca is processed root powder with starch reduced by gelatinization. The manufacturer positions it for vitality; it adds a food-and-plant-nutrient matrix rather than caffeine, a complete protein serving or a direct hormone replacement.
  • L-Carnitine (15mL liquid): Fatty acid transporter — shuttles fat into mitochondria for energy. The liquid is the recorded serving format; the function is fatty-acid transport.
  • Vital Greens (20mL): Concentrated greens in separate glass. Micronutrient density to start the day — do not mix with protein shake.

Every day used, 04:00 priming. The wake-up stack — protein + collagen + adaptogens before the day starts.

Collagen: source, type and purpose

Marine means fish-derived; bovine means cattle-derived. The recorded PROMISE Peptan marine product is type I. Source alone does not establish superiority or identify every type in another product.

Type I provides tensile structure in skin, tendons and bone; type II is associated with cartilage. Hydrolyzed peptides provide amino-acid building blocks for connective-tissue turnover. Undenatured type II is a different formulation and purpose, not the current peptide serving.

Collagen complements muscle-protein foods: its amino-acid pattern is different from whey, casein and the beef-protein matrix.
