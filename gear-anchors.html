The actual rig, band-by-band and handle-by-handle — straight from the PROJ3K_100 equipment manifest, not stock photos.

Bars, harnesses & extension straps

  • Tribe Lifting Modular Resistance Bar

    3 PIECES · 360° SWIVELS

    Purpose: Swivel rings let the attachment rotate as the bar moves.

    Usage: Use for the recorded band setup with controlled, even loading.

    Design & specifications

    Polished chrome knurled bar with three threaded sections and independent rotating ball-bearing swivel rings at both ends.

  • HPYGN Heavy Pilates Structural Bar

    Foam-padded steel

    Purpose: A foam-covered bar provides a broad hand-contact surface.

    Usage: Use for supported band movements with the documented terminal straps.

    Design & specifications

    Black steel bar covered in dense non-slip black foam. Its nylon terminal straps have distinctive red edge stitching.

  • THEFITGUY Ultimate Extension Straps

    Dual D-ring straps

    Purpose: D-ring straps extend the working attachment point.

    Usage: Use to position the handle or band for the recorded setup.

    Design & specifications

    Flat black nylon extension straps with silver steel D-rings at both ends. Bright red zigzag and box reinforcement runs along their faces; the black fabric tag reads thefitguy.

  • THEFITGUY Sled Pulling Belt & Straps Kit

    2 × 9.8 FT · 4 HOOKS

    Purpose: The belt and pulling straps connect the sled to the waist.

    Usage: Use for controlled sled pulls with the listed kit connections.

    Design & specifications

    Wide padded black neoprene waist harness with two steel D-rings, two 9.8-foot pulling straps and four silver oval snap hooks. Red diamond and X stitching identifies the belt and straps.

Connection hardware & bundle sleeves

  • Oak-Sports Heavy-Duty Carabiners

    25 kN · 5,600 LB

    Purpose: Screw-lock carabiners connect rated hardware in the load path.

    Usage: Close and lock the gate; align the load along the spine.

    Design & specifications

    Bright silver symmetrical oval steel frames with black textured screw-lock gates. The equipment manifest lists a 25 kN / 5,600 lb rating.

  • JRSGS Rock Locking Clips

    25 kN · 2 VARIANTS

    Purpose: Gate-lock clips close the connection between strap, band and handle.

    Usage: Check the gate and load orientation before every set.

    Design & specifications

    Asymmetric matte-black D-shaped frames with screw-lock barrels. Variant A has a metallic-red barrel; Variant B has a matte-black barrel. The manifest lists 25 kN.

  • Neoprene Cable / Bundle Management Sleeves

    1.5 IN

    Purpose: Flexible sleeves keep multi-band arrays together and shield contact points.

    Usage: Wrap the selected bundle without covering inspection points.

    Design & specifications

    Flexible black wraps enclosing multi-band arrays. The reversible split-seam sleeves use a continuous hook-and-loop closure for bundle management and contact shielding.

Anchor infrastructure

  • VEHICLEX Axle Tie-Down Straps

    Sleeved webbing

    Purpose: Sleeved webbing brings an anchor point to a D-ring terminal.

    Usage: Use the documented anchor arrangement and inspect the webbing.

    Design & specifications

    Thick black webbing with gold/silver triangular steel D-rings and sliding protective sleeves. A red rectangular patch with white VEHICLEX lettering identifies the sleeve.

  • NILIGHT Soft Loop Anchor Straps

    1.7 × 17 IN

    Purpose: Webbing loops create a non-marking connection around the structural anchor.

    Usage: Use at the recorded anchor point and inspect the loop before loading.

    Design & specifications

    Short black polyester choke-loop straps with repeating white X/diamond reinforcement. The small white/black/red warning tag carries the Nilight emblem.

Additional items in the lived inventory

  • XSTRAP Standard Motorcycle Soft Loop Tie-Down Straps

    Soft Loop

    Purpose: A second webbing-loop option provides non-marking anchor contact.

    Usage: Use only in the documented setup; inspect the webbing and connections.

    Design & specifications

    Woven soft-loop anchor straps, originally built for motorcycle tie-down duty — repurposed as a second non-marking anchor-strap option alongside the Nilight set.

Equipment identities and listed ratings follow the supplied PROJECT 100 visual recognition database. Tube ratings, combined set ratings and force at a particular stretch are different measurements.
