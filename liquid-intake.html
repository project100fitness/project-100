CARD 01

V3 RECOVERY

WHEN  Daily anchor; optional when consumed on rest days

Base

Cold filtered water; shaker volume unspecified

Ingredients · 6
  1. MuscleMeds Carnivor Beef Protein Isolate (33.9 g Canadian scoop)
  2. PROMISE Pure Marine Collagen Peptan 10 g
  3. Rootalive Organic Gelatinized Black Maca 5 g
  4. ALLMAX Liquid L- Carnitine 1500 (15 mL / 1,500 mg)
  5. MegaFood C Defense Gummy (1, chewed)
  6. NAKA Platinum Vital Greens (20 mL separate flush)
Protocol

Purpose: Morning protein anchor to help meet daily protein needs and supply amino acids for tissue maintenance and recovery.

Composition:

  • Beef isolate is the main protein source; marine collagen adds complementary peptides.
  • Maca adds a plant-based component; L-carnitine participates in fatty-acid transport for energy metabolism.

Usage:

  • 04:00 on the recorded daily schedule; optional when consumed on rest days.
  • Mix with cold water.
  • Chew the gummy and take the greens separately.

CARD 02

V1 IGNITION

WHEN  Morning training only (State A)

Base

500-600 mL cold filtered water

Ingredients · 6
  1. ALLMAX Impact Igniter Xtreme 2.0 (2 scoops / 28 g)
  2. ALLMAX MuscleEAA Xtreme (1 scoop / 17.73 g)
  3. PeakO2 2,000 mg
  4. Betaine Anhydrous 2,500 mg
  5. Citrulline Malate 2:1 (4-6 g)
  6. Pure Taurine Powder (2-3 g). Citicoline 250 mg capsule taken separately.
Protocol

Purpose: Morning pre-workout preparation: caffeine supports alertness and exercise readiness; essential amino acids supply protein-building substrates.

Composition:

  • Igniter 2.0 and MuscleEAA form the base, with PeakO2, betaine, citrulline malate and taurine.
  • The blend brings amino acids and workout-focused ingredients into one morning preparation routine.

Usage:

  • Before morning training, in 500–600 mL cold water.
  • Citicoline stays a separate capsule.
  • V1 and V6 are alternative branches; never combine them on the same day.

CARD 03

V2 REFUEL

WHEN  During the training session (States A and C)

Base

600-750 mL cold filtered water

Ingredients · 4
  1. Perfect Sports BCAA HyperClear (1 scoop / 7.5 g BCAAs 2:1:1)
  2. RapiDrem Electrolyte Powder (1 scoop, about 6.5 g)
  3. Kirkland Himalayan Pink Salt 1/16 tsp [0.3125 mL]
  4. 2-3 Medjool dates chewed solid, separately
Protocol

Purpose: During-session fluid intake, with sodium for electrolyte replacement and dates for carbohydrate energy.

Composition: Water, RapiDrem electrolytes, Himalayan pink salt and BCAA HyperClear. BCAAs supply leucine, isoleucine and valine, while the separate dates provide carbohydrate fuel.

Usage:

  • Sip 600–750 mL during the workout.
  • Chew 2–3 dates separately.
  • Timing follows the session on both morning and evening training days.

CARD 04

V4 RELOAD

WHEN  After the workout (States A and C); R-ALA remains separate

Base

350-450 mL room-temperature water

Ingredients · 4
  1. BioSteel Recovery Protein Plus (1 scoop / 36 g half-serving)
  2. Rootalive Beetroot Powder 7 g raw
  3. Proline Pure Creatine HCl 750 mg active
  4. Kirkland Himalayan Salt 1/16 tsp [0.3125 mL]

Whole R-ALA 300 mg is taken separately before the shaker.

Protocol

Purpose: Post-workout protein intake to support repair and help meet daily nutrition targets.

Composition: BioSteel protein, beetroot, PRO LINE creatine HCl and pink salt. Creatine participates in muscle energy metabolism; beetroot adds plant nutrients, and pink salt supplies sodium.

Usage: After the session, in 350–450 mL room-temperature water. Take the recorded whole R-ALA capsule separately before the shake.

CARD 05

V5 TRANSPORT

WHEN  Rest days only (State B)

Base

500 mL cold filtered water

Ingredients · 5
  1. Proline Pure Creatine HCl 750 mg
  2. Collagen — type unresolved
  3. ALLMAX Liquid L-Carnitine 1500 (15 mL / 1,500 mg)
  4. RapiDrem Electrolyte Powder (1 scoop)
  5. Kirkland Himalayan Salt 1/16 tsp [0.3125 mL]
Protocol

Purpose: Rest-day fluid and nutrient product anchor, without a pre-workout stimulant blend.

Composition: PRO LINE creatine HCl, collagen (type unresolved), liquid L-carnitine, electrolytes and pink salt. Collagen adds complementary protein peptides, electrolytes supply minerals, and water supports daily hydration.

Usage: Rest branch only, around 16:00–16:30, in 500 mL cold water. Keep the recorded portions; do not substitute it for the training-day vectors.

Further study: Portions, label arithmetic & estimate uncertainty →

Source difference · V5 collagen

V5 collagen type is unresolved: Guidebook V3.7 and the summary card say bovine; the Encyclopedia V3.6 detailed card and T1.0 gap log G-V5 say marine. The recorded amount is 1 scoop, about 10 g. Owner confirmation is pending; neither type is presented as a settled current instruction.

CARD 06

V10 SHUTDOWN

WHEN  Night anchor across all three states

Base

800 mL Traditional Medicinals Hibiscus Tisane, comfortable temperature

Ingredients · 5
  1. SD Pharmaceuticals Creatine HCl 750 mg
  2. North Coast Naturals Fermented L-Glutamine 5 g
  3. AOR Pure Glycine Powder 3-5 g
  4. Bovine collagen 1 scoop, about 10 g
  5. optional half-scoop Medi-C Plus (2.5 g)
  6. rare Niyama Hey Relax full scoop
Protocol

Purpose: Warm night-time drink that closes the daily routine and carries the recorded evening ingredients.

Composition:

  • Hibiscus tisane, SD Pharmaceuticals creatine HCl, glutamine, glycine and bovine collagen.
  • Glycine and glutamine add amino acids, collagen contributes protein peptides, and warm hibiscus provides the base for a consistent evening routine.

Usage:

  • 18:30 reference time across the daily branches.
  • Use 800 mL comfortably warm tisane in a hot-drink thermos.
  • Optional ingredients remain optional, as listed.

CARD 07

V6 PUMP

WHEN  Evening training only (State C); replaces V1

Base

500–600 mL cold filtered water

Ingredients · 7
  1. ALLMAX Impact PUMP Xtreme (Blue Raspberry): 2 scoops / 31 g
  2. ALLMAX MuscleEAA Xtreme (Cherry Limeade): 1 scoop / 17.73 g
  3. Compound Solutions / ALLMAX PeakO2: 2,000 mg by mass
  4. NOW Betaine Anhydrous (TMG): 2,500 mg
  5. ALLMAX Essentials Citrulline Malate 2:1: 4–6 g
  6. ALLMAX Essentials Pure Taurine Powder: 2–3 g
  7. AOR Xerenoos Citicoline: 1 capsule / 250 mg, swallowed separately with water

No added salt or creatine HCl in this shaker.

Protocol

Purpose: Evening pre-workout preparation without added caffeine in the recorded Pump formula.

Composition:

  • ALLMAX PUMP and MuscleEAA, plus PeakO2, betaine, citrulline malate and taurine.
  • EAAs supply essential amino acids; citrulline participates in nitric-oxide production, and betaine contributes to cellular water balance.

Usage:

  • 20–30 minutes before evening training, in 500–600 mL cold water.
  • Citicoline stays separate at T−30. V6 replaces V1; never use both on the same day.
