The five chapters

The chapters of the return.

5 chapters
  1. 01 2010–2013

    The Beginning

    Age 30. No logs, no photos — pure acquisition. Learning to lift, eat, and recover from a standing start.

  2. 02 2013–2016

    Progressing

    First meal logs and training records begin. Fitness becomes a stated, primary goal — habits solidify.

  3. 03 2016–2018

    The Golden Era

    Highest-output window of the first build. SMM increased, partitioning tightened — precise and ambitious.

  4. 04 2018–2025

    The Interruption

    A difficult personal period interrupted regular routines. The return begins with a fresh start and a repeatable day.

  5. 05 2025–2026+

    The Comeback

    More knowledge and precision than any prior era. Target: 100 lb SMM, sustained below 15% BF — permanence, not a peak.

Origins

The journey began in 2010. A difficult personal period later interrupted regular routines. A fresh start brought renewed attention to health, training and recovery in 2026. Earlier dated baselines remain part of the documented journey.

"The Pivot occurred at 45. 197.1 lb total body weight, 88.2 lb skeletal muscle mass, 22.9% body fat. These numbers didn't indicate dysfunction — they indicated equilibrium."

Mindset
