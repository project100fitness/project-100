Sixteen years in the making — not two.

The full story →

2010 start, leanest point on record in 2018 (14.4% body fat), an interruption, then the 2026 comeback.

Why this goal is personal

Training has been part of my life since 2010. An interruption broke the structure I had built; the comeback is about rebuilding that structure with a clearer understanding of my body, my limits and the work I can repeat.

At 46 and 170 cm, reaching 100 lb of skeletal muscle while reducing body fat below 15% is a challenge against my own starting physiology. Age, frame, training history, recovery and the practical constraints documented in FIT PROTOCOL shape every decision.

The ambition is substantial. The recorded 88.2 lb muscle estimate leaves 11.8 lb to the target — about 13.4% above that baseline. Those figures describe the task; they do not promise a result or prove a population ranking. The record below keeps the earlier chapters, dated measurements and photographs together.

The five chapters

The chapters of the return.

5 chapters
  1. 01 2010–2013

    The Beginning

    • Age 30. No logs, no photos — pure acquisition.
    • Learning to lift, eat, and recover from a standing start.
  2. 02 2013–2016

    Progressing

    First meal logs and training records begin. Fitness becomes a stated, primary goal — habits solidify.

  3. 03 2016–2018

    The Golden Era

    Highest-output window of the first build. SMM increased, partitioning tightened — precise and ambitious.

  4. 04 2018–2025

    The Interruption

    A difficult personal period interrupted regular routines. The return begins with a fresh start and a repeatable day.

  5. 05 2025–2026+

    The Comeback

    More knowledge and precision than any prior era. Target: 100 lb SMM, sustained below 15% BF — permanence, not a peak.
