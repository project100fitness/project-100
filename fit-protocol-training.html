6 · Training Mechanics and Movement Classes

Training Programme

Movement classes are defined by direction of force, joint angles and loading demands - distinct from intake vectors V1-V10.

Supported rows, presses and machine-based work dominate where hardware locks require spinal protection. Free axial loading is progressed conservatively.

  • Tempo, setup repeatability and pain response are recorded for every primary movement.
  • The 2026 ACSM update supports individualized resistance training prescription; it does not endorse any single Project 100 template for every reader.

Hinge (modified)Reduced-range hinge as toleratedSymptom-guided; stopped by neurological signs.

Analysis 6A - load management. Hypertrophy requires progressive mechanical tension with adequate recovery. Volume landmarks are individual: the correct dose is the largest dose that leaves performance stable or improving across the week with hardware locks quiet.

Soreness alone is not a progress metric.

Daily guide notes · Training — Analysis

Training — Analysis

Progressive overload with joint protection — the training lock.

MECHANISM

Muscle is built by progressive mechanical tension plus sufficient protein and energy, and kept by repeating that signal before the previous adaptation fades.

  • At 46, the synthesis side still works; the recovery side is the constraint.
  • The program therefore buys growth with volume you can recover from, not volume you can survive once.

STACK INTERACTION

  • With stimulant vectors: caffeine (IGNITER portion ~373 mg before tea or other sources; Earl Grey adds roughly 40–70 mg) raises output and masks fatigue — the mask is the risk.
  • RPE must be logged honestly even on high-caffeine days.

With sleep: a short night downgrades the next session automatically. No vector compensates for that downgrade.

With joints: load progression is capped by joint feedback, not by target weights.
