Folder 02

Illustration pack

The illustration pack holds 133 pictures in 17 styles: 19 overview boards and 114 individual cards (seven vectors, seven pill stages, three day branches and the gym steps). Each picture has a download link for its full-size original on the page where it appears.

Pictures are illustrations. The recipe text beside them is what governs doses, timing and handling. The register lists every picture with its style, subject and source pages.
