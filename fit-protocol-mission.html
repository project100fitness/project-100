The mission

Main site: Goals →

100 lb is the stretch. 90–93 lb of true muscle is where I judge myself, with body fat below 15% as the second marker.

  • 01OpenThree readings of one goal100 stretch · 92–97 on the scan · 90–93 true tissue+
The mission

Three readings of one goal

90–93lb true SMM — my validated contractile corridor, the dry tissue I can realistically build and keep.
92–97lb on a BIA printout — what a standardized InBody scan may read when I am fully hydrated.
100lb disclosed stretch — kept openly as ambition, never presented as a promise or a deadline.

My target is 100 lb of skeletal muscle mass (SMM) with body fat below 15%, at 5 ft 7 in (170 cm) and age 46, drug-free. I keep 100 on the cover because it drives the work. I judge progress in the corridor underneath it.

Train with intent. Eat with a record. Rest without guilt. Let symptoms — not ambition — set the ceiling on any given day.

Body fat below 15% is my personal marker, not a medical threshold for health. A bigger session or a bigger meal does not prove better adaptation. Durability does.

Where I stand

Why the scan reads high

Bioelectrical impedance does not weigh dry muscle fibers. It estimates skeletal muscle mass from intracellular water conductivity. Everything that pushes water into myocytes moves the printout — with no new contractile protein required.

What inflates my readout, item by item

DriverMy doseWhat it does on the scan
Creatine HCl1.5 g per day (2 x 750 mg)Draws water into muscle cells; the device counts that water as SMM
Betaine anhydrous (TMG)2,500 mg (V1 and V6)Osmolyte — holds intracellular water, supports methylation
Taurine3-4 g per day across vectorsSarcoplasmic swelling and bile-acid conjugation; adds to cell volume
Potassium from RapiDrem1,000 mg per scoopPrimary intracellular cation — shifts the water compartment the scan measures
Post-workout carbohydrate19 g in V4 plus mealsGlycogen holds ~3 g water per gram; a full glycogen store reads heavier
Net BIA effect+2 to 4 lb SMMWhy 94-98 lb on paper can sit on top of 91-93 lb of dry tissue

My body, my constraints

After gallbladder removal

I am post-cholecystectomy: my liver still makes bile — it simply trickles instead of storing it. Smaller fat portions, slower eating and meal enzymes fit me better than one large fatty meal.

A historical note of steatosis is part of why liver enzymes stay on my periodic laboratory watch (Ch. 14).

My body, my constraints

Sleep and age

At least seven hours of sleep opportunity, consistent wake and caffeine timing. A late stimulant is never a remedy for poor sleep.

These are my constraints, recorded from my history. They explain my exercise choices; they are not advice for another person's condition.

A day in the system

Three states, one rule

StateWhenWhat runsWhat stays off
A — Morning gymMy standard training dayV3 04:00 — V1 06:00 — V2 in session — V4 after — V10 18:30V5, V6
B — RestOff-gym recoveryV3 (if used) — V5 16:00 — V10 18:30V1, V2, V4, V6; lunch and afternoon pill grids
C — Evening gymSchedule-shift dayV3 — V6 before evening training — V2 in session — V4 after — V10 at nightV1 that morning, V5

The one rule that protects all three: V1 and V6 never run on the same day — V1 xor V6 = 1. Morning stimulation and evening pump work each have their place; together they would double the cholinergic, taurine, betaine and citrulline load and cost me sleep.

On a State C day the morning citicoline primer does not run — one 250 mg capsule moves to T-30 minutes before V6.

A day in the system

Why three states exist

State A (morning gym), State B (rest) and State C (evening gym) are not scheduling trivia; they are three different chemistries sharing one 24-hour clock.

Design questionState A answerState B answerState C answer
IgnitionV1 (caffeine ~373 mg) at 06:00None — de-escalation is the pointV6 (0 mg caffeine) in the evening
Cholinergic primerCiticoline 250 mg at 05:30OffCiticoline 250 mg at T-30 before V6
Creatine pulsesV4 750 mg + V10 750 mgV5 750 mg + V10 750 mgV4 750 mg + V10 750 mg (V6 base adds 1,000 mg native monohydrate)
Pill gridsFull: wake, first-meal, lunch DMZ (10), afternoon (5), nightWake pack + enzymes with meals only; lunch/afternoon grids skippedFull training grids, timed to the evening session
Night closeNight stage + V10 18:30, sodium-freeSame close — foundations do not take days offSame close; V6's zero-caffeine design protects it

The rest day is the clearest statement of the system: food stays, enzymes stay, foundations stay — stimulation, pump chemistry and the training grids leave. De-escalation is designed, not accidental.

Philosophy

Durability outranks comparison

The singularity is not a physique borrowed from a stage. It is the point where measurement, training, digestion, sleep and laboratory honesty work as one system — sustained for years.

My scoreboard is my own comparable measurements, my function and my health. Quarterly review beats weekly overreaction. One change, one review window, one decision.

Project 100 — 100 lb of skeletal muscle mass, body fat below 15% — remains the horizon I work toward, disclosed honestly as a stretch. The corridor beneath it is where I keep score.

The method underneath both is unglamorous and repeatable: measure honestly, train repeatably, eat with a record, sleep, and let symptoms set the ceiling.

Age 46 — Project 100 Encyclopedia V3.2 — 6 October 2026

Personal record. Not medical advice.

Folder 06

The story

Main site: The story →

Sixteen years in the making — not two: a stated 2010 start, a leanest point on record in 2018 (14.4% body fat), an interruption, and the 2026 comeback.

  • 01OpenThe five chapters2010 to 2026+: from a standing start to the comeback+
The story

The five chapters

  1. 2010–2013 · The Beginning. Age 30. No logs, no photos — pure acquisition. Learning to lift, eat and recover from a standing start.
  2. 2013–2016 · Progressing. First meal logs and training records begin. Fitness becomes a stated, primary goal — habits solidify.
  3. 2016–2018 · The Golden Era. Highest-output window of the first build. SMM increased, partitioning tightened — precise and ambitious.
  4. 2018–2025 · The Interruption. A difficult personal period interrupted regular routines. The return begins with a fresh start and a repeatable day.
  5. 2025–2026+ · The Comeback. More knowledge and precision than any prior era. Target: 100 lb SMM, sustained below 15% body fat — permanence, not a peak.
