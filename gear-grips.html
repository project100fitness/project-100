Grip & Handle Accessories

Equipment manifest →

Tricep ropes, ergonomic handles, stirrups, pull-up grips and multi-grip handles.

The actual rig, band-by-band and handle-by-handle — straight from the PROJ3K_100 equipment manifest, not stock photos.

Grips & cable attachments

  • BLUSLM Ergonomic Tricep Rope

    Hand Rope-03

    Purpose: Twin rope arms offer separate hand positions for cable-style extensions.

    Usage: Use for controlled triceps extensions and press-downs.

    Design & specifications

    Extra-long twin black braided nylon arms, a chrome connection collar and large molded black palm-flare stops.

  • HXD-ERGO Biomechanical Handles

    Curved

    Purpose: Curved handles offer an alternative grip for pulling and pressing.

    Usage: Select a comfortable wrist angle and a controlled range.

    Design & specifications

    Asymmetric black ergonomic grip molds, flat nylon straps with a blue-and-white shield logo, and integrated fixed black carabiners.

  • INNSTAR High-Traction Stirrup Handles

    Honeycomb

    Purpose: Honeycomb grips provide hand contact for band and cable-style movements.

    Usage: Use as paired handles for rows, presses and isolation work.

    Design & specifications

    Triangular black nylon stirrup handles with black carabiners. The ABS grip cores have anti-slip silicone honeycomb texture; white INNSTAR lettering runs along the straps.

  • Angles90 Camo Pull-Up Grips

    A90

    Purpose: Curved handles and slings allow the hands to rotate during pulling.

    Usage: Use for pull-ups and rows with a comfortable grip orientation.

    Design & specifications

    Bright orange curved handles on flat black nylon slings, with black A90 GRIPS lettering along the outer curve.

  • THEFITGUY Single-Arm Tricep Rope

    16.5 IN

    Purpose: A single rope lets each arm work independently.

    Usage: Use for single-arm triceps extensions and controlled press-downs.

    Design & specifications

    Single-arm black braided rope with a zinc-plated top snap hook. The distinctive red bell-shaped palm stop carries debossed thefitguy lettering.

  • MANUEKLEAR Neoprene Multi-Grip Handles

    3 GRIP ZONES

    Purpose: Sewn grip pockets offer three hand positions in one attachment.

    Usage: Choose the pocket that fits the movement and comfortable reach.

    Design & specifications

    Extra-wide matte-gray neoprene/nylon straps with one silver D-ring. Sewn upper, middle and lower grip pockets provide three positions; the side tag has a yellow-and-white logo.

Additional items in the lived inventory

  • SELEWARE Non-Slip Pull-Up Power Balls

    Grip Balls

    Purpose: Ball grips create a crush-grip challenge during pulling.

    Usage: Use for controlled grip work and forearm finishers.

    Design & specifications

    Textured rubber ball grips that mount to any bar or rig anchor — forces a crush-grip through every rep instead of a flat palm, built for finishing off grip and forearm work.

  • Vulken Tricep Rope Cable Attachment

    Green

    Purpose: A second braided rope option provides paired hand contact.

    Usage: Use for triceps press-downs and cable-style rope movements.

    Design & specifications

    Braided nylon rope with molded rubber stops on each end — a second rope option in the rotation alongside the red BLUSLM and single-arm THEFITGUY ropes.

  • Rauuueo Ergonomic Neutral Grip Pull-Up Handles

    Neutral Grip

    Purpose: Angled handles offer a neutral hand position.

    Usage: Use for rows and pull-ups within a comfortable range.

    Design & specifications

    Angled neutral-grip handles that mount to the pull-up bar — keeps the wrists in a natural position through pull-ups and rows instead of a straight overhand grip.

Equipment identities and listed ratings follow the supplied PROJECT 100 visual recognition database. Tube ratings, combined set ratings and force at a particular stretch are different measurements.
