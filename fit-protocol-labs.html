Part VI · Laboratory Integrity

Playbook rules

  • March 2026 laboratories predate the May supplement stack. Never cite them for or against the current stack.
  • Record creatine, biotin and caffeine on the laboratory requisition note or patient portal message.
  • Creatine kinase (CK) after hard training is expected to rise; time draws away from peak sessions when CK interpretation matters and tell the clinician the training date.
  • Any troponin concern bypasses supplement reasoning entirely: emergency assessment first.
  • Monitoring plan: quarterly standardized BIA and waist; sleep, digestion and performance logs weekly; laboratory panel timed to clinical need with the confounders above disclosed. That cadence converts this Protocol reference from a record into a surveillance system.

Daily guide notes · Labs and Monitoring — Analysis

Labs and Monitoring — Analysis

WHY YOUR LABS NEED CONTEXT

There is no universal 72-hour biotin hold: disclose it and follow assay-specific instructions. Second, creatine HCl (1.5 g daily) expands the creatinine pool, so kidney checks should include cystatin-C alongside creatinine.

March laboratory results predate the May supplement stack — do not read them as a verdict on the current stack. An afternoon testosterone result is non-diagnostic; hormone interpretation needs two fasting morning draws.

THE DAILY MONITORING MINIMUM

  • Sleep duration and quality; morning HRV where available
  • Session RPE and completion, honest on stimulant days
  • GI tolerance (the osmotic stack announces itself here first)
  • Mood and headache (the cholinergic stack announces itself here first)
