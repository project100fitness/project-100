Load path & safety →

The load path & rated hardware

  • A handful of items in this rig aren't just gym accessories — they're the same class of hardware used in mountain-climbing protection and heavy-vehicle tie-down: forged, gate-locking carabiners and high-tensile webbing straps built to industrial breaking-strength standards.

Here's how they're actually rigged into the setup, and the checks that keep that load path safe.

  1. 1

    Structural Anchor

    The rig's welded steel crossbar, or a non-marking soft-loop strap choked directly around it.

  2. 2

    Tie-Down Strap

    VEHICLEX / THEFITGUY webbing extends the anchor point out to a forged steel D-ring terminal.

  3. 3

    Locking Carabiner

    A JRSGS 25kN gate-lock clip closes the chain — gate faced away from the pull, locked shut.

  4. 4

    Band / Handle

    The working resistance — tubing, a grip handle, a rope attachment — clips in last, closest to the load.

  5. 5

    You

    The working end of the chain — only ever as strong as the weakest link rigged in behind it.

Climbing & Vehicle-Rated Hardware

  • JRSGS Rock Locking Clips

    25 kN · 2 VARIANTS

    Purpose: Gate-lock clips close the connection between strap, band and handle.

    Usage: Check the gate and load orientation before every set.

    Design & specifications

    Asymmetric matte-black D-shaped frames with screw-lock barrels. Variant A has a metallic-red barrel; Variant B has a matte-black barrel. The manifest lists 25 kN.

    Climbing-Grade Rating

  • VEHICLEX Axle Tie-Down Straps

    Sleeved webbing

    Purpose: Sleeved webbing brings an anchor point to a D-ring terminal.

    Usage: Use the documented anchor arrangement and inspect the webbing.

    Design & specifications

    Thick black webbing with gold/silver triangular steel D-rings and sliding protective sleeves. A red rectangular patch with white VEHICLEX lettering identifies the sleeve.

    Vehicle-Rated Webbing

  • THEFITGUY Ultimate Extension Straps

    Dual D-ring straps

    Purpose: D-ring straps extend the working attachment point.

    Usage: Use to position the handle or band for the recorded setup.

    Design & specifications

    Flat black nylon extension straps with silver steel D-rings at both ends. Bright red zigzag and box reinforcement runs along their faces; the black fabric tag reads thefitguy.

    Aircraft-Grade Webbing

  • NILIGHT Soft Loop Anchor Straps

    1.7 × 17 IN

    Purpose: Webbing loops create a non-marking connection around the structural anchor.

    Usage: Use at the recorded anchor point and inspect the loop before loading.

    Design & specifications

    Short black polyester choke-loop straps with repeating white X/diamond reinforcement. The small white/black/red warning tag carries the Nilight emblem.

    Non-Marking Anchor

  • XSTRAP Motorcycle Soft Loop Tie-Downs

    Soft Loop

    Purpose: A second webbing-loop option provides non-marking anchor contact.

    Usage: Use only in the documented setup; inspect the webbing and connections.

    Design & specifications

    Woven soft-loop straps built for securing a motorcycle in transit — a second non-marking anchor option alongside the Nilight set.

    Vehicle-Rated Webbing

Advantages

  • Every anchor point breaks down and reconfigures in seconds — nothing bolted or permanently mounted.
  • Soft-loop and tie-down straps don't mark or wear the structural steel the way a bare hook or band would.
  • Locking gates prevent the accidental unclipping a plain resistance-band loop is prone to mid-set.
  • Rated in kN / lbs, so the hardware's actual failure point sits well above anything the bands can pull.

Safety Measures

  • Match hardware rating to the heaviest band in the line with real margin — never eyeball it as "strong enough."
  • Carabiner gate faces away from the direction of pull, fully closed and locked, checked before every set.
  • Webbing inspected before use — any cut, fraying, or UV-bleaching gets that strap retired immediately, no exceptions.
  • No daisy-chaining mismatched hardware — every link in the setup should carry its own independent rating.
  • Carabiners loaded along the spine only — side- or gate-loading drops rated strength dramatically.
  • No shock-loading — bands are let out under control, never snapped or dropped into a sudden stop.

Ratings above reflect the manufacturer specs printed on the hardware itself. This is a home-gym rig, not a certified climbing or industrial rigging system — so those numbers are treated as a floor to stay well under, not a target to push toward.
