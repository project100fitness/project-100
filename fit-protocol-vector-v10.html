V10 SHUTDOWN

Night anchor across all three states

Night anchor (18:30 reference); morning training, rest and evening-training branches. Tisane at a comfortable temperature.

800 mL Traditional Medicinals Hibiscus Tisane, comfortable temperature

  • SD Pharmaceuticals Creatine HCl 750 mg
  • North Coast Naturals Fermented L-Glutamine 5 g
  • AOR Pure Glycine Powder 3-5 g
  • optional half-scoop Medi-C Plus (2.5 g)
  • rare Niyama Hey Relax full scoop

Protocol

Purpose: Warm night-time drink that closes the daily routine and carries the recorded evening ingredients.

Composition: Hibiscus tisane, SD Pharmaceuticals creatine HCl, glutamine, glycine and marine collagen. Glycine and glutamine add amino acids, collagen contributes protein peptides, and warm hibiscus provides the base for a consistent evening routine.

Usage: 18:30 reference time across the daily branches. Use 800 mL comfortably warm tisane in a hot-drink thermos. Optional ingredients remain optional, as listed.

Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
Nocturnal recovery: second creatine pulse, 5 g glutamine, 3-5 g glycine. Optional Medi-C adds 1,000 mg vitamin C and 105 mg magnesium; Hey Relax adds 200 mg magnesium and 1,000 mg inositol. The old 60-65 degree Celsius heating rule is retired.
Stack interaction
Magnesium 341-646 mg + high- dose vitamin C + continuous bile trickle — Loose stools are predictable system output; log and moderate fat bolus before blaming single foods.
Margin
Supplemental magnesium: training baseline 341 mg; up to 646 mg with options, versus 350 mg/day supplemental UL. Rest totals are lower and not fully quantified.
Monitor
Stool, pain, weakness, sleep and performance noted; options marked as taken or skipped.
Stop rule
Recurrent watery stools with weight loss, oily stools or significant abdominal pain: stop and seek assessment.

Ingredient facts

  • Creatine HCl (750mg): Second recorded HCl serving, following V4. Creatine supports the phosphocreatine energy buffer used during brief, intense efforts; the evening position completes the recorded dose split.
  • Glutamine (5g fermented): Glutamine carries nitrogen and supplies fuel for intestinal and immune cells. Fermentation describes the ingredient’s production; its recorded evening placement is separate from the complete meal-protein matrix.
  • Glycine (3-5g): Glycine contributes to collagen and glutathione synthesis and inhibitory nerve signaling. The recorded evening serving serves the wind-down purpose; it is a separate amino acid rather than a complete protein.
  • Marine Collagen (10g PM): The recorded evening marine peptides provide glycine, proline and hydroxyproline for connective-tissue turnover. Source, collagen type and hydrolysis are separate properties; this is not a replacement for complete meal protein.
  • Hibiscus Tisane (800mL): Calming herbal tea base. Comfortable temperature — the ritual signals wind-down. Antioxidant-rich, caffeine-free.

Daily anchor, 18:30. The shutdown sequence — no stimulants, focused on recovery, gut health, and sleep preparation.

Collagen: source, type and purpose

Marine means fish-derived; bovine means cattle-derived. The recorded PROMISE Peptan marine product is type I. Source alone does not establish superiority or identify every type in another product.

Type I provides tensile structure in skin, tendons and bone; type II is associated with cartilage. Hydrolyzed peptides provide amino-acid building blocks for connective-tissue turnover. Undenatured type II is a different formulation and purpose, not the current peptide serving.

Collagen complements muscle-protein foods: its amino-acid pattern is different from whey, casein and the beef-protein matrix.
