# Personal goal: research and arithmetic

Reviewed 21 October 2026. Main explanation: project-goals.html#goal-physiology. Detailed research is toward the end, after the main modules.

## Janssen et al. (2000): MRI muscle distribution, 468 adults aged 18–88

Cross-sectional age differences; absolute muscle decline more apparent toward the end of the fifth decade. Height and weight explained about half the variation. Not an age-46 growth forecast or a matched percentile table.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10904038/

## ACSM position stand (2026): 137 reviews, more than 30,000 participants

Progressive resistance training improves muscle size and function; higher weekly volume enhanced hypertrophy. Does not forecast this individual’s gain, deadline or tolerance of the existing 15-exercise day.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843416/

## Murphy & Koehler (2022): energy deficit and resistance training

Lean-mass gains reduced during energy deficiency; meta-regression estimated zero gain around a 500 kcal/day deficit. Group estimate, not a personal cutoff.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34623696/

## Morton et al. (2018): protein and resistance training, 49 studies / 1,863 participants

Estimated protein breakpoint 1.62 g/kg/day, 95% CI 1.03–2.20; outcome fat-free mass, not pure skeletal muscle. Supplements added 0.30 kg mean FFM relative to comparator, not total training gain.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/

## Longland et al. (2016): four-week controlled trial in young men

20 per group; about 40% energy deficit, intensive exercise, 2.4 versus 1.2 g/kg/day protein. Higher-protein group gained more lean mass and lost 4.8±1.6 kg fat versus 3.5±1.4 kg. Not a forecast or recommended regimen for a 46-year-old.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26817506/

## Janssen et al. (2000): BIA equation validated against MRI in 388 adults

Equation standard error 2.7 kg (9%). This historical equation’s population error is not the owner’s InBody error bar or repeatability limit.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10926627/
