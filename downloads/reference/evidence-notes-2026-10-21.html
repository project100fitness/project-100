DETAILED STUDY / REVIEWED 21 OCTOBER 2026

Understand the mechanics, measurements and evidence

Use this reference alongside the full technique and recipe cards. Recorded personal setups, general scientific findings and illustrative calculations answer different questions; the sections below keep them separate.

Band mechanicsMuscle rolesTraining ProgrammeNutrition measurementsIngredientsBiometrics
01 / MECHANICS

Band tension is not joint loading

Force
Elastic tubing tests measure force at specified elongations and starting lengths. A colour or stock rating alone does not describe the force throughout a repetition. Tubing tension study.
Joint moment
External torque magnitude is force × perpendicular moment arm: τ = F × r × sin θ. Force and line angle both matter. OpenStax, torque.

Worked example / hypothetical, not a measured personal load

A 50 N pull with a 0.25 m perpendicular moment arm produces 12.5 N·m external torque. If the perpendicular arm falls to 0.10 m at the same force, torque is 5 N·m. Real band force also changes as the band length changes. The hardest joint position cannot be identified from band stretch alone.

What to record for a repeatable setup

  • Band make, count, unloaded length and visible wear.
  • Anchor height, body distance and support contacts.
  • Start and finish positions, range and whether the line stays taut.
  • Actual measured tension if available; otherwise label the force as unmeasured.

For this collection, these are documentation variables, not instructions to exceed the equipment’s rated extension or the owner’s restrictions. A limb drawing supplies no calibrated force measurement.

02 / ANATOMY

Read muscle roles without inventing activation scores

Main / red
The intended joint action described in the personal guide.
Secondary / blue
Other movers and local joint control described in that setup.
Support / green
Postural effort and the actual seat, foot, hand or knee contacts.

Pressdown example

Triceps brachii extends the elbow. Its long head also crosses the shoulder, so shoulder position belongs in the explanation. The review by Landin and colleagues examines triceps function across shoulder and elbow positions. It does not provide an activation percentage for this owner’s seated band setup. Triceps function review.

What the illustration cannot establish

Surface EMG amplitude is not a validated stand-in for later muscle growth. A colour overlay is even further removed: it is an explanatory drawing, not an EMG recording, force measurement or tissue scan. Missing colour does not prove that a muscle is inactive. Vigotsky et al., interpretation of EMG and hypertrophy.

Joint-control references

AAOS shoulder and spine conditioning guides describe controlled positioning and muscle roles. Their rehabilitation exercises require appropriate individual assessment; linking them does not certify every illustrated band variation for a person with spinal hardware. AAOS shoulder programme · AAOS spine programme.

03 / TRAINING

A programme is a recorded dose, not a promised outcome

The 2026 ACSM position stand synthesized 137 systematic reviews involving more than 30,000 participants. Progressive resistance training improved muscle size and function; higher weekly volume enhanced hypertrophy. Average findings do not establish an individual deadline, tolerance of 15 exercise slots, or a requirement to take every set to failure. ACSM 2026 position stand.

Plan
Sets, repetitions, exercise order and the intended movement.
Execution
Actual repetitions, technique, range, assistance and load used.
Response
Session effort, symptoms, recovery and subsequent performance.
Interpretation
Compare repeated conditions before calling a change progress. A source video is not verification of every planned set.

The detailed cards retain the original form checks and easier options. Personal stop rules and constraints still govern the recorded plan.

04 / NUTRITION

From cups and spoons to a defensible nutrient estimate

A label serving is a reference amount; a portion is the amount actually eaten. FDA labels pair an appropriate household measure with a metric amount. NIDDK explains that consuming two label servings doubles the listed calories and nutrients. FDA serving-size explanation · NIDDK portions and servings.

Calculation / illustrative label

If a label gives 5 g protein per 30 g food, a weighed 45 g portion supplies 45 ÷ 30 × 5 = 7.5 g protein. This example is arithmetic, not a value assigned to any salad photograph.

Mass and volume
Cups, tablespoons and teaspoons describe volume. Grams describe mass. A universal cup-to-gram or scoop-to-gram conversion cannot cover every ingredient.
Product match
Use the actual product’s label and serving basis. Keep drained, undrained, raw and cooked weights distinct.
Recipe total
Scale each ingredient to its stated amount, sum the nutrients, then allocate the portion. Include dressing once.
Uncertainty
Unweighed portions and ambiguous toppings justify ranges. Decimal places do not turn a photograph into a measured meal.

The site’s existing quantities and nutrient models remain the recipe record. These notes explain how to interpret them, rather than silently changing ingredient weights.

05 / INGREDIENTS

A biological mechanism is not proof of a finished formula

NIH’s Office of Dietary nutrient products reviews ingredient-specific performance evidence. Creatine has support for repeated high-intensity activity; evidence for product-derived arginine is limited and conflicting, while citrulline findings are insufficient for a firm performance conclusion. Beta-alanine results depend on the task and study protocol. These are different evidence profiles, not interchangeable “performance” claims. NIH ODS performance-nutrient product review.

Mechanism
The proposed biological pathway and whether it has actually been demonstrated in humans.
Trial result
The tested ingredient, formulation, duration, participants and measured outcome.
Recorded formula
The exact products, amounts and timing on the vector card; evidence for a single ingredient does not establish the full stack’s result.
Personal response
Actual tolerability, sleep, symptoms and performance. A stimulant effect or a pump does not quantify muscle growth.

NIH notes that multi-ingredient products complicate interpretation. It also discusses adverse effects and interactions; research findings are not permission to add ingredients or raise the recorded doses. NIH ODS safety and evidence sections.

06 / MEASUREMENTS

Compare the same quantity under comparable conditions

The goals research distinguishes skeletal muscle estimates from fat-free mass, an individual’s change from a population rank, and arithmetic from a prediction. It includes primary references on age, resistance training, energy deficiency, protein and measurement uncertainty.

  • Retain the original dated report and device/method.
  • Keep measured results separate from estimates and targets.
  • Document conditions that could alter the comparison.
  • Use performance and the dated photographic record alongside body-composition readings.
