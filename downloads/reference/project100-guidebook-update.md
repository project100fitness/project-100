## Read nutrition numbers with their serving basis

Each food record needs the actual recipe or source ID, the amount prepared, the amount eaten and the nutrition basis. Whole blend, whole plate, three wraps, per jar and a whole dressing batch are different quantities. Keep caption-reported figures labelled as caption reported. Keep recipe-model estimates labelled as modeled. Missing values remain unknown.

The salad collection now has 14 ingredient models. Four eggs apply to the egg-led bowl without beans or lupini; two eggs apply to the mixed-bean and lupini version. Tuna-and-bean bowls without eggs or lupini use a whole can of beans, provisionally 240 to 360 g drained. These are modeling rules, not measurements from the photographs.

Heavy dressing uses avocado and avocado oil. Light dressing uses olive oil and glaze and describes texture rather than a lower energy value. The cards include the full prepared dressing batch. If dressing is left in the bowl, actual consumption is lower by an unmeasured amount; leftover volume alone does not provide a reliable calorie deduction.

The meal stories retain nine records, some with shared source posts or reconstructed amounts. The owner estimate of 80 to 110 g protein describes a whole fudge bowl, not a single rice-cake sandwich. Switching protein milk to almond milk changes the calculation and requires the new product label. [S03 S04 S06]

The three-day focus collection uses the long-line resistance-band system. The two full-body days use the matched Pilates kit only. The current interactive programme presents twelve distinct exercise cards per active day. This is the programme layout, not proof of recorded working-set totals.

Pilates band labels and counts match on the left and right. The owner-confirmed configuration is two equal bands per side, four total. Your personal trainer handles resistance and individual adjustments. Nominal bundle sums identify equipment and do not measure force at the hands.

Use the exercise page for current technique cards and session downloads. Use Kinetics for equipment, attachments and recorded setup context. Source passages document observed movement phases and are not a substitute for individual technique review. [S01 S02 S08]


## Use the three muscle roles correctly

Primary means the intended movement contribution, secondary means assistance and joint control, and support means postural stabilization and base contacts. These are qualitative roles. They do not report activation percentages, and a stabilizing region should not be described as doing the same work as the main driver. [S01]
