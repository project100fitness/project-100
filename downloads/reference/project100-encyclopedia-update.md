## How the Kinetics analysis was structured

The analysis proceeds from media inventory to screening evidence, short observation windows, consolidated movement groups, anatomical interpretation and publication records. Each layer answers a different question. Inventory establishes which files were present. Sampling locates candidate actions. Sequential screening improves coverage within a selected recording. Source geometry checks resolve contacts and hardware when a smaller screen was ambiguous. Consolidation prevents repeated exports or posture changes from becoming invented exercise families.

The current source catalogue retains a stable phase ID, source filename, start and end time, movement family, body position, anchor location, pull direction, hand path, equipment description and classification. A source window describes the passage that supported the interpretation. It does not establish that the passage begins at the first working repetition or ends at the last one. [S02]

Geometry analysis separates the visible attachment from an assumed resistance system. A sleeve or black line alone does not prove elastic tubing. A rigid bar held overhead can support a bodyweight movement. Webbing with buckles can be suspension equipment. A handle clipped to a cable stack remains cable-loaded even if the grip resembles band hardware. These distinctions drove the exclusion of several superficially similar movements from the band-only edition. [S01 S18]

Support contacts are recorded separately from the limb action: both knees on floor, knees on a padded box, bench straddle, seated box, deep squat, staggered stance or a free hand braced on a support. An occluded knee or hand is left uncertain. A box adaptation is identified as an editorial adaptation when the recorded source actually used a bench. [S01 S02]

Hand topology is a separate field. Two independent handles maintain separate load paths. Two hands on one handle share one grip. A rigid bar joins its two ends mechanically. A rope can change hand orientation without becoming a second independent resistance circuit. Joining two pictured handles into a single impossible band path is a rendering error, not an alternative interpretation.

The source refinement log documents corrections rather than deleting inconvenient evidence. In particular, shared grips were separated from independent grips; a low squat was distinguished from kneeling; a lateral weight shift was not renamed a straight-leg Cossack squat; free-hand gestures and walking resets were retained as evidence rather than prescribed exercises. [S01]


## Resistance systems force and movement interpretation

KUZARO long-line bands and the HPYGN short-band Pilates kit are distinct systems. The owner also uses a Tribe Lifting three-piece bar and rotating eyelets, PLYOSOFT padded boxes and several handles, ropes, straps and carabiners. Brand and ownership records identify the equipment; they do not establish that every observed line in every recording is that product. [S08 S09]

High, middle and low describe relative anchor zones rather than measured heights. The resistance direction runs from the working attachment back toward its anchor. The hand movement may travel away from that anchor during the loaded phase. A single upward or downward arrow is insufficient unless its meaning is stated. Source descriptions use relative directions because numerical anchor coordinates and pulling angles were not measured. [S01 S02]

Turning effect depends on the force, its direction and the perpendicular distance from the joint or pivot. Therefore identical nominal bands can create different joint demands when body position or the line of pull changes. The recordings contain no calibrated force measurements or complete three-dimensional joint coordinates, so this compilation reports no personal torque values. General principle reference R02; personal geometry records [S01 S02].

Nominal label totals identify bundles. They are not constant hand force, measured joint load or a hardware working-load limit. Current data do not supply a calibrated force-extension curve, resting and stretched lengths for each repetition, dynamic force trace or anchor load measurement. No numeric tension curve is inferred from the footage.

For parallel elastic lines, the contribution at the hand depends on the actual tensions and their directions. Merely adding labels omits extension, routing, slack and geometry. Two left and right circuits also do not establish the force at one hand by adding both sides. The source values are retained as nominal bundle arithmetic only.

The confirmed Pilates arrangement uses identical band labels and counts on the left and right, with two equal bands per side and four total. The recorded label options are 45, 55 and 65 lb. These are equipment descriptions, not levels or assigned training loads. The personal trainer handles resistance, range and progression. Old level-specific fields are not carried into this reference as prescriptions. [S01 S08]

Both full-body programme days are Pilates-kit-only in the current publication. Six newer kit adaptations expand technique variety without creating six new personal video sightings. The master reference preserves the difference between an accepted technique, a source-observed variant and an editorial kit adaptation. [S01]

## Muscle roles and anatomical interpretation

The primary role identifies muscles expected to contribute directly to the interpreted joint action. The secondary role identifies assistants and local joint control. The support role identifies postural stabilization and support contacts. These roles are qualitative and depend on the actual movement; they are not a three-rank strength score or an electromyography result. [S01]

A compound sequence can combine elbow motion, shoulder motion and a torso hinge. Naming the sequence only for biceps or triceps would hide the other components. Conversely, a small torso movement does not establish that every visible back or leg region is a major driver. The recorded action, joint path and base must be described before assigning a muscle emphasis.

Green support emphasis is intentionally restrained in the current visual standard. A planted leg, seated pelvis or braced hand supplies support but does not imply maximal activation throughout the whole limb. No percentage activation or exact deep-muscle boundary can be read from a surface illustration. This text reference carries the same limitation even though it contains no artwork. [S01]

The observed library covers chest and pressing, back and pulling, front, side and rear shoulder actions, elbow flexion and extension, forearm and grip control, lower-limb and hip patterns, and trunk support or movement. This is a taxonomy of the reviewed library, not proof that every muscle was directly isolated or trained equally. The family-level records and individual variants below preserve those distinctions.

### Serving basis and accounting

Prepared batch means all ingredients modeled for the entire bowl or blend. Consumed serving means the quantity actually eaten. Per jar, whole blend, three wraps and whole plate are different denominators. The basis must stay beside every calorie or macronutrient number. Missing total carbohydrate must remain unknown even when a caption states net carbohydrate or fibre.

For a homogeneous measured mixture, a serving calculation can multiply the batch nutrition by the serving mass divided by total batch mass. Layered salads, selectively eaten toppings and separated oil are not necessarily homogeneous. Leftover liquid volume alone cannot establish a valid energy deduction when oil, vinegar and food particles separate. [S03 S04 S06]

The current salad model has three display scenarios: base without dressing, heavy avocado dressing and light olive-oil and glaze dressing. Light describes texture. The model can assign more energy to the light dressing because of the oil and glaze quantities. No lower-calorie claim follows from the name. [S03 S06]

Portion rules are explicit: an egg-led bowl without mixed beans or lupini uses four eggs; a mixed-bean and lupini bowl uses two eggs; tuna with beans and no eggs or lupini uses one whole can of beans. Whole-can drained weight is provisional at 240 to 360 g until the package is available. A 15-inch bowl does not establish mass, fill depth or a consumed serving. [S03 S06]

The model sums each coefficient multiplied by grams divided by 100. Its low and high endpoints combine assumed low and high ingredient quantities; they are scenario bounds, not statistical confidence intervals. Energy coefficients can differ from a simple protein/carbohydrate/fat energy cross-check because the model uses rounded generic coefficients. A mismatch should trigger reconciliation, not silent replacement of a reported number.

Sodium, sugars, saturated fat and numeric micronutrient totals are not established for these bowls without labels and verified portions. Missing values are not zeros. Product variation matters particularly for feta, olives, pickled ingredients, canned foods, protein powders, milk and glaze. The historical plan of 2780 kcal, 252 g protein and 69 g fat remains a planning reference rather than verified daily consumption. [S03 S04 S06 S07 S10]

## Consolidated findings and unresolved questions


### Coverage is broad but not exhaustive

The archive contains 740 inventoried files and several review layers. The frozen edition has 18 completed representative long-file screenings, one partial and 24 pending. Do not equate compiled guides with exhaustive review of all footage.


### Observed passages are not workout volume

The 173 windows identify evidence. They do not establish 173 sets, repetitions, training days or separate exercise types. Twelve cards on a programme day are planned presentation slots rather than video-verified volume.


### Hardware identity changes interpretation

Cable, suspension webbing, exposed elastic tubes and rigid bar support can look similar in a cropped frame. Several early names changed after source geometry review. Preserve those corrections alongside the retained evidence.


### Support is part of the movement description

Floor kneeling, box kneeling, bench support and low squatting create different bases. An obscured contact remains unresolved. Changing a bench to a box in an illustration is an adaptation, not a new claim about the recording.


### Anatomy is interpreted rather than measured

Muscle roles follow the visible action and known anatomy. No recording-based activation magnitude, muscle-growth result or injury tolerance is established by this project data.


### Portion basis drives nutrition meaning

Full blend, whole plate, per jar and whole dressing batch are incompatible serving denominators. Keep each basis beside the number. Shared ingredients or a related source post do not validate a reconstructed bowl.


### Unknown toppings must stay unknown

The current salad model excludes several unidentified proteins. Reintroducing a guessed tuna or chicken entry would inflate confidence and distort both recipe and nutrition totals.


### Historical publications need a correction pass

Current web sources have corrected several older book claims. The new supplements supply an explicit replacement map. The archived PDFs should remain visibly dated until a complete editorial and clinical review produces a genuinely revised full edition.


### Calibration and label work remain open

No force-extension curve, measured joint torque, complete package-label inventory or weighed whole-day diet is supplied. Those are separate future measurement tasks. This reference does not invent their results.


### One data source should feed every derivative

The same accepted record IDs should populate Encyclopedia explanations, Guidebook summaries, website cards and downloads. Nutrition scenarios and evidence status must travel with each derivative. A count or title correction should not become a new observation.


## A common analysis structure for future chapters

| Lens | Nutrition application | Kinetics application |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | What the ingredient or preparation contributes, with serving basis | Which visible joint action and line of pull are being interpreted |
| Interaction | What is counted in another meal, dressing or liquid recipe | How grip, support, torso action and anchor combine |
| Margin | How wide the portion assumptions and missing-label uncertainty are | Which geometry, hardware and force quantities are unmeasured |
| Monitor | Weights, labels, batch yield and the quantity actually consumed | Source ID, support contact, attachment and trainer feedback |
| Review or stop rule | Which missing input prevents a numeric nutrition claim | Which unresolved contact or equipment type prevents a training interpretation |

The current protocol site uses five lenses named Mechanism, Stack interaction, Margin, Monitor and Stop rule. For this editorial reference they are retained as an analysis pattern, without inventing new medical thresholds. An editorial review rule means a claim is held until evidence is available; it is not a newly prescribed clinical action. [S13]
