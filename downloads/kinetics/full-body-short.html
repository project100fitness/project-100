Day 5 / Full body A / moderate

01

Shoulder band-bar squat

Legs and hips / 2 planned sets / 8-12

START / standing FINISH / partial squat

GRIP / CONTACT / Overhand / pronated grip. Retain the illustrated overhand grip with thumbs wrapped and wrists aligned. Palm direction follows the arm position.

SETUP / Padded bar across upper back; paired low side/rear lines.

MOVE / Bend hips and knees into a controlled partial squat.

RETURN / Extend hips and knees toward standing.

MAIN / RED

Quadriceps and hip extensors contribute to standing from the squat.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Hamstrings and spinal extensors assist hip and trunk control; shoulder and arm muscles stabilize the bar.

SUPPORT / GREEN

Abdominal muscles stabilize the torso, with feet planted on the floor. Ankle and calf muscles control the base.

Technique study / Shoulder band-bar squat

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
A short padded bar lies across the upper back, held at both ends. Paired elastic lines travel down toward low side/rear rack points.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Stand between the attachments with the bar supported across the upper back and both hands holding it.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Bend hips and knees into a controlled partial squat.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Extend hips and knees toward standing.
FORM CHECKS
Keep the trunk steady, wrists aligned and the movement controlled.
EASIER OPTION
Use a smaller comfortable range and reduce band tension.
EXTERNAL PULL DIRECTION
Down/side toward the paired low anchors
EQUIPMENT / LOAD RECORD
HPYGN Pilates / matched pairs

Reading the muscle colours: Colour is qualitative surface projection, not measured muscle activation or exact tissue boundaries. Some regions are projected beneath clothing. Deep or occluded assistants are explained in the text; absence of a visible colour patch does not mean absence of muscular work.

03

Seated single-leg band curl / Pilates pair

Legs and hips / 1 planned set / 8-12 each side

START / heel forward FINISH / heel drawn back

GRIP / CONTACT / Ankle-cuff resistance. Resistance attaches to the illustrated ankle cuff; the hands provide support only.

SETUP / Stable box; low front line and approved ankle cuff; two matching bands on the working side.

MOVE / Draw the heel back by bending the knee.

RETURN / Return slowly; keep the heel path clear of the box.

MAIN / RED

Hamstrings bend the knee, drawing the heel back.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Gastrocnemius can assist knee flexion. Ankle-positioning muscles hold the foot position; hip muscles keep the thigh aligned.

SUPPORT / GREEN

box, opposite planted foot and trunk muscles steady the pelvis. The working thigh stays supported.

Technique study / Seated single-leg band curl / Pilates pair

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
Sit at the front edge of a stable PLYOSOFT box. Attach the ankle cuff to the low front line.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Sit tall with the working thigh supported. Start with that heel forward and the knee softly extended; keep the ankle comfortable.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Exhale and slide or draw the heel back by bending the knee. Keep the thigh and pelvis quiet rather than pulling with the hip.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Keep the heel path in front of the box. Extend the knee slowly without sudden recoil.
FORM CHECKS
If the PLYOSOFT box shifts, cuff slips, knee hurts or pelvis rocks, stop and reset; use less tension.
EASIER OPTION
Rehearse the heel slide without a band. Do not add resistance until the cuff and anchor are appropriate.
EXTERNAL PULL DIRECTION
Forward toward the low front anchor
EQUIPMENT / LOAD RECORD
Individually selected long-line bands; KUZARO stock ceiling applies.

06

PLYOSOFT box-supported one-arm row / Pilates pair

Back / 2 planned sets / 8-12 each side

START / softly extended arm FINISH / controlled pull

GRIP / CONTACT / Neutral handle grip. Palms face inward relative to the working arm; keep wrists in line with forearms.

SETUP / Stable box; one handle on a front line near hand height; two matching bands on the working side.

MOVE / Draw the elbow toward the lower ribs without twisting.

RETURN / Lower slowly to a softly extended arm.

MAIN / RED

Latissimus dorsi: draws the upper arm back toward the trunk.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Rhomboids and middle trapezius control the shoulder blade; rear deltoid and elbow flexors assist the pull.

SUPPORT / GREEN

Abdominals and spinal stabilizers resist rotation. Hips, thighs and ankle muscles steady both feet; the other arm supports the torso.

Technique study / PLYOSOFT box-supported one-arm row / Pilates pair

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
Use a stable PLYOSOFT box and a secure front rack anchor near the working hand's starting height. Clip one handle to one continuous pull line; keep the recoil path clear.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Plant both feet in a comfortable stagger. Support the opposite palm under its shoulder. Incline from the hips; keep neck and trunk aligned, knees soft.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Gently brace while breathing. Exhale and guide the working elbow toward the lower ribs; keep the shoulder away from the ear.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Stop before the torso twists, the shoulder rolls forward or the elbow has to travel far behind the body. Return slowly to a softly extended arm.
FORM CHECKS
Both feet and the support hand stay planted. Shorten the pull or reduce resistance if you shrug, jerk or rotate.
EASIER OPTION
Rehearse unloaded, use less tension, or use a more upright supported row if the hinge is uncomfortable.
EXTERNAL PULL DIRECTION
Toward the front rack anchor
EQUIPMENT / LOAD RECORD
Individually selected long-line bands; KUZARO stock ceiling applies.

07

Supported single-arm chest press / Pilates pair

Chest and shoulders / 2 planned sets / 8-12 each side

START / handle beside chest FINISH / controlled press

GRIP / CONTACT / Neutral handle grip. Palms face inward relative to the working arm; keep wrists in line with forearms.

SETUP / Stable box; rear anchor near chest height; two matching bands on the working side.

MOVE / Press forward without leaning or twisting.

RETURN / Stop before elbow lock; return within a comfortable arc.

MAIN / RED

Pectoralis major: moves the upper arm forward and across the chest.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Triceps extend the elbow; anterior deltoid assists. Scapular muscles guide the shoulder blade.

SUPPORT / GREEN

Trunk muscles resist rotation; the box and planted feet stabilize the pelvis and legs.

Technique study / Supported single-arm chest press / Pilates pair

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
Use a stable non-rolling PLYOSOFT box and a secure anchor behind you near chest height. Keep the band beside the arm, away from the neck and face.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Sit tall with both feet planted. Start with the handle beside the chest, neutral wrist and elbow below shoulder height. Let the shoulder blade move naturally.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Breathe out and press forward smoothly while keeping ribs and pelvis steady. Do not lean or twist to finish.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Finish before the elbow locks; return only as far as the shoulder remains comfortable. Avoid a deep forced stretch behind the torso.
FORM CHECKS
If the ribs flare, shoulder pinches or trunk turns, shorten the range and lower the load.
EASIER OPTION
Use an unloaded press rehearsal or a comfortable wall press-up instead.
EXTERNAL PULL DIRECTION
Back toward the rear chest-height anchor
EQUIPMENT / LOAD RECORD
Individually selected long-line bands; KUZARO stock ceiling applies.

GRIP / CONTACT / Neutral handle grip. Palms face inward relative to the working arm; keep wrists in line with forearms.

SETUP / Sit on the red box; clip two equal Pilates tubes to the low line and active handle. Match the opposite bundle.

MOVE / Bend the elbow without swinging or leaning back.

RETURN / Lower slowly to a softly extended elbow.

MAIN / RED

Brachialis, biceps brachii and brachioradialis bend the elbow.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Forearm muscles hold a neutral grip; shoulder muscles steady the upper arm.

SUPPORT / GREEN

box, feet and trunk control keep the pelvis and torso still.

Technique study / Seated neutral-grip curl / Pilates pair

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
Use one light handle line from a secure low anchor in front, clear of the feet. A light handheld weight is an alternative.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Sit tall with both feet planted. Keep the working upper arm beside the body, thumb up and wrist straight.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Breathe out and bend the elbow without swinging the upper arm forward or leaning back.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Curl through a comfortable arc, then lower slowly to a softly extended elbow. Keep the band away from the face.
FORM CHECKS
Avoid wrist bending, shoulder hiking or a tightening painful grip. Reduce resistance if control changes.

Arms and core / 1 planned set / 8-12

START / elbows bent FINISH / soft elbow extension

GRIP / CONTACT / Overhand / pronated grip. Retain the illustrated overhand grip with thumbs wrapped and wrists aligned. Palm direction follows the arm position.

SETUP / Sit on the red box; two equal Pilates tubes attach to each bar end from the high front mount.

MOVE / Extend the elbows while keeping upper arms nearly still.

RETURN / Bend the elbows slowly; keep the bar clear of the face.

MAIN / RED

Triceps brachii: straightens the elbow.

SECONDARY / BLUE

Forearm muscles steady the grip and wrist; shoulder muscles hold the upper arms beside the torso.

SUPPORT / GREEN

Trunk muscles hold a quiet seated posture; both feet and the box provide support.

Technique study / Seated bar pressdown / Pilates pairs

SETUP / EQUIPMENT
Use one secure high rack anchor, one rated pull line and a straight-bar attachment. Position a stable PLYOSOFT box so the line stays in front of the body.
START POSITION / SUPPORT
Plant both feet; sit tall. Keep upper arms beside the ribs and wrists in line with forearms. Choose a grip you can hold without strain.
WORKING MOVEMENT
Exhale and extend the elbows without pushing the shoulders down with body weight. Keep the upper arms nearly still.
RANGE / CONTROLLED RETURN
Stop short of snapping the elbows straight. Return gradually into comfortable elbow flexion without letting the bar rise toward the face.
FORM CHECKS
Avoid a rocking torso, bent wrists or moving the whole upper arm. Reduce load if the elbows or grip lose control.
EASIER OPTION
Rehearse the elbow motion unloaded or use a lighter single-handle variation matched to current tolerance.
EXTERNAL PULL DIRECTION
Up/front toward the high rack anchor
EQUIPMENT / LOAD RECORD
Individually selected long-line bands; KUZARO stock ceiling applies.
