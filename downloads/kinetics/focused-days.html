Days 1–3: focused sessions

DAY 1 / SESSION

Push / shoulder control

Chest, elbow extensors and comfortable shoulder control

Read each linked technique guide before starting. Warm up and rehearse unloaded; check the base, breathing and return.

  1. 1

    Supported single-arm chest press

    Main exercise2 sets8-12 each side repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit tall with both feet planted. Start with the handle beside the chest, neutral wrist and elbow below shoulder height. Let the shoulder blade move naturally.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Pectoralis major: moves the upper arm forward and across the chest.
  2. 2

    Seated straight-bar pressdown

    Supporting exercise1 set8-12 repetitions

    Get into position

    Plant both feet; sit tall. Keep upper arms beside the ribs and wrists in line with forearms. Choose a grip you can hold without strain.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Triceps brachii: straightens the elbow.
  3. 3

    Short-arc shoulder raise

    Easy control1 set6-10 easy repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit tall, feet planted, elbows softly bent and hands relaxed. Start with arms beside the body.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Middle deltoid contributes to lifting the arm out to the side.
  4. 4

    Seated brace with normal breathing

    Easy control1 set3 relaxed breaths per hold; 3 holds repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit comfortably tall with ribs over pelvis and both feet on the floor. Keep shoulders relaxed and avoid arching the back.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Abdominal wall: develops gentle trunk stiffness while breathing.

Effort: about four more good repetitions still possible. Unloaded control drills stay easy; do not force fatigue. Rest 60-120 seconds, or longer as needed.

DAY 2 / SESSION

Lower body / supported base

Knee and hip extension, hamstrings, calves and lateral hip control

Read each linked technique guide before starting. Warm up and rehearse unloaded; check the base, breathing and return.

  1. 1

    Supported sit-to-stand

    Main exercise2 sets6-10 repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit near the front with feet about hip-width apart, heels planted and knees following the toes. Use armrests for assistance if needed.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Quadriceps and gluteus maximus raise the body from the chair.
  2. 2

    Seated single-leg band curl

    Main exercise2 sets8-12 each side repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit tall with the working thigh supported. Start with that heel forward and the knee softly extended; keep the ankle comfortable.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Hamstrings bend the knee, drawing the heel back.
  3. 3

    Supported calf raise

    Supporting exercise1 set8-12 repetitions

    Get into position

    Stand with feet about hip-width apart, weight shared between them and knees soft. Hold support lightly but securely.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Gastrocnemius and soleus lift the heels.
  4. 4

    Supported side leg lift

    Supporting exercise1 set6-10 each side repetitions

    Get into position

    Stand tall with the pelvis level and stance knee soft. Keep toes facing mostly forward.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Gluteus medius and minimus lift the thigh away from the midline.

Effort: about four more good repetitions still possible. Unloaded control drills stay easy; do not force fatigue. Rest 60-120 seconds, or longer as needed.

DAY 3 / SESSION

Pull / arm control

Back pulling, shoulder-blade control and elbow flexors

Read each linked technique guide before starting. Warm up and rehearse unloaded; check the base, breathing and return.

  1. 1

    Bench-supported one-arm row

    Main exercise2 sets8-12 each side repetitions

    Get into position

    Plant both feet in a comfortable stagger. Support the opposite palm under its shoulder. Incline from the hips; keep neck and trunk aligned, knees soft.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Latissimus dorsi: draws the upper arm back toward the trunk.
  2. 2

    Supported shoulder-blade control

    Easy control1 set6-10 easy repetitions

    Get into position

    Keep neck long, ribs settled and shoulders relaxed. Avoid starting in an exaggerated chest-out posture.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Middle trapezius and rhomboids gently retract the shoulder blades.
  3. 3

    Seated neutral-grip curl

    Supporting exercise1 set8-12 each side repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit tall with both feet planted. Keep the working upper arm beside the body, thumb up and wrist straight.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Brachialis, biceps brachii and brachioradialis bend the elbow.
  4. 4

    Seated brace with normal breathing

    Easy control1 set3 relaxed breaths per hold; 3 holds repetitions

    Get into position

    Sit comfortably tall with ribs over pelvis and both feet on the floor. Keep shoulders relaxed and avoid arching the back.

    See setup & four movement steps →
    Which muscles are working?Abdominal wall: develops gentle trunk stiffness while breathing.

Effort: about four more good repetitions still possible. Unloaded control drills stay easy; do not force fatigue. Rest 60-120 seconds, or longer as needed.
