YOUR PRINTABLE WEEKLY PLAN

Your week.
One day at a time.

Three focused workouts. A rest day. Two full-body sessions. Another rest day.

1 Choose a level2 Find today's session3 Follow the exercise guide

1. Choose your starting level

A repetition is one complete movement. A set is a group of repetitions before a rest. Main exercises and supporting exercises are marked in each session.

LEVEL 1

Start with less

2 main sets · 1 supporting set

Finish while you could still do 4 more good repetitions.

LEVEL 2

Add supporting work

2 main sets · 2 supporting sets

Finish while you could still do 3 more good repetitions.

LEVEL 3

Add main-exercise work

3 main sets · 2 supporting sets

Finish while you could still do 2 more good repetitions.

These are the focused-day settings. Control drills stay easy. Day 5 uses no more than two sets; day 6 is one easy set, leaving at least five more good repetitions possible, or take a rest day. Level numbers do not prescribe band weights.

3. Set up before you move

  1. Set your base. Place your feet and support hand as shown.
  2. Brace gently. Keep breathing and keep knees and elbows soft.
  3. Move, then return slowly. Follow the comfortable range in the exercise sheet.

Stop for pain or lost control. Open the exercise sheet before trying a new movement.
