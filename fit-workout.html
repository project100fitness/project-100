Five lenses on training: mechanism, stack interaction, margin, monitoring and the stop rule.

Mechanism
Progressive mechanical tension with adequate recovery.
Stack interaction
Stimulant vectors can mask fatigue; sleep and joint response govern the next session.
Margin
Volume landmarks are individual; no fixed template is endorsed for every reader.
Monitor
Record load, RPE, setup, pain and recovery.
Stop rule
Stop for progressive weakness, new numbness or bowel/bladder change.
