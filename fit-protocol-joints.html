Chapter 10 - Connective tissue and injury considerations

Collagen, vitamin C, glycine and load progression support tolerance; no supplement proves nerve healing.

Daily collagen exposure is substantial and deliberate: 10 g marine collagen in V3, 10 g in V5 on rest days,

plus bovine collagen in legacy meal modules and glycine 3-5 g plus glutamine 5 g in V10. Vitamin C (including optional Medi-C) is the required co-factor for collagen cross-linking. The system pairs substrate

with progressive, symptom-guided loading - the stimulus that actually directs tissue adaptation.

Elbow, shoulder and spinal history require exercise substitution rather than bravado. Supported positions,

controlled tempo and range limited by symptoms are the connective-tissue strategy. Tart cherry and milk thistle in the night stage are recorded as used; their contribution cannot be isolated from sleep and load

management.
