Why the vectors work

One line per vector

  • Every vector carries one pathway line beside its count, dose and benefit rows.
  • The line explains the system; the rows in Chapter 8 prove it.
  • The two nitric-oxide routes — citrulline in V1/V6, beetroot nitrate in V4 — are kept distinct on purpose.

  • V1 Ignition. Why this works:

    • Citrulline feeds the arginine-nitric-oxide pathway for blood flow
    • Citicoline plus the alpha-GPC in the formula feed acetylcholine for focus and contraction
    • Caffeine blocks adenosine to cut perceived fatigue
    • Beta-alanine builds muscle carnosine to buffer acid as sets get hard
    • Betaine (TMG) donates methyl groups in the methionine cycle while helping cells hold water
    • Formula nootropic matrix inside the Igniter base (DMAE 500 mg, Huperzine-A 200 mcg, tyrosine 2,000 mg, choline 400 mg, theanine 400 mg, bacopa 320 mg, cocoa 500 mg, juniper 200 mg — counted, never standalone pills); and a separate cholinergic primer (citicoline 250 mg capsule).

    Creatine is held out of the shaker.

  • V6 Pump.

    • Why this works: nitric-oxide hyperemia without stimulants — citrulline 8,000 mg + added malate, NitroVascine (inositol-stabilized arginine silicate) 250 mg, L-arginine 500 mg (as sulphate), GlycerPump 2,000 mg for hyper-hydration, plus osmolyte symmetry (betaine 5,000 mg on V6 days: 2.5 g native + 2.5 g standalone) and Alpha-GPC inside the base formulas only.

    Zero caffeine; 220 mg formula sodium and 1,000 mg native creatine monohydrate counted in the base.

  • V2 Refuel. Why this works: sodium and potassium maintain fluid balance and nerve-to-muscle signaling during the session, magnesium and calcium support contraction chemistry, and the leucine in the 2:1:1 BCAAs signals the mTOR pathway that switches muscle protein synthesis on.
  • V3 Recovery. Why this works: beef isolate supplies the amino acids muscle rebuilds with; collagen provides glycine and proline, the building blocks of connective tissue; and L-carnitine shuttles fatty acids into the mitochondria, where they can be burned for energy.

  • V4 Reload. Why this works:

    • Beetroot nitrate takes a second, separate route to nitric oxide (nitrate to nitrite to NO) from the citrulline route in V1/V6
    • BioSteel's carbohydrate and protein refill glycogen and start repair
    • Creatine tops up the phosphocreatine store that re-makes ATP between sets
    • R-ALA, taken separately as a whole capsule, supports glucose handling and antioxidant recycling
  • V5 Transport. Why this works: on a rest day the job is maintenance — creatine keeps the phosphocreatine store full for tomorrow, carnitine keeps fatty-acid transport moving, collagen keeps supplying connective-tissue building blocks, and the RapiDrem electrolytes keep fluid balance steady without a training stimulus.

  • V10 Shutdown. Why this works:

    • Glycine is an inhibitory neurotransmitter and a collagen building block, associated with calmer, cooler sleep
    • Glutamine is a preferred fuel for gut and immune cells
    • Creatine quietly maintains the phosphocreatine store overnight
    • The optional Medi-C Plus vitamin C is the cofactor the body needs to turn collagen amino acids into actual collagen

Lens 1:

  • One cholinergic capsule (citicoline 250 mg) plus formula-native cholinergics inside the pre-workout bases (Ch. 22)
  • Creatine is held out of the V1 shaker so the pump chemistry stays auditable
  • V6 carries 1.0 g native monohydrate counted in the base

Lens 2:

  • Alpha-GPC is counted only where it actually enters — inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day 500 mg)
  • DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg are counted where they actually enter — inside the V1 Igniter formula
  • Huperzine-A 50 mcg inside the V6 Pump formula

Lens 5:

  • Cholinergic load is capped by architecture (V1/V6: at most one on training days; neither on rest days; citicoline sole capsule; the two formula Huperzine-A doses never stack same-day, half-life ~10-24 h) rather than by willpower — the firewall is the safety mechanism.

Why the vectors work

Five lenses on the pathways

Lens 1 — Physiology.

  • The pathway map is deliberately non-redundant: one cholinergic capsule (citicoline 250 mg) plus formula-native cholinergics inside the pre-workout bases (Ch. 22), one adrenergic vector (V1), one stimulant-free NO vector (V6), one methylation/osmolyte anchor (betaine, both ignition modes), and night inhibitory chemistry (magnesium glycinate, glycine, tart cherry).

Each mechanism has a single primary owner so dose accounting (Ch. 12) stays arithmetic, not guesswork.

Lens 2 — Measurement. Pathway claims are tied to countable payloads:

  • Citrulline totals are stated as free-base equivalents (10.7-12 g), betaine is brand-and dose-locked (NOW Sports, 2,500 mg), and Alpha-GPC is counted only where it actually enters — inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day 500 mg)
  • DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg are counted where they actually enter — inside the V1 Igniter formula
  • Huperzine-A 50 mcg inside the V6 Pump formula

A mechanism without a counted dose does not appear in this chapter.

Lens 3 — Training. Timing is mechanism:

  • Citicoline at 05:30/T-30 precedes the session it supports
  • R-ALA precedes the V4 shake and is read against its 19 g carbohydrate context
  • The 10:30 band deload sits between session and reload as blood-flow volume that does not become a second workout

Pathways are scheduled, not just listed.

Lens 4 — Nutrition & digestion.

  • The post-cholecystectomy gut shapes pathway choices: taurine's bile-conjugation role, meal enzymes with every solid meal, chewed dates instead of blended intra-workout carbohydrate, and conditional liver items (Liver-GI, milk thistle) that support conjugation without pretending to replace a gallbladder.

Lens 5 — Laboratory & safety.

  • Piperine (5 mg) is the chapter's honest double-edge: it raises curcumin exposure by slowing clearance, and that same clearance-slowing is why it is watched (Ch. 13).
  • Cholinergic load is capped by architecture (V1/V6: at most one on training days; neither on rest days; citicoline sole capsule; the two formula Huperzine-A doses never stack same-day, half-life ~10-24 h) rather than by willpower — the firewall is the safety mechanism.

Two compounds inside my formulas

My position

DMAE (dimethylaminoethanol).

  • A choline analog (DMAE bitartrate, typically 500-2,000 mg/day).
  • Human evidence is thin and old; a related compound failed cognitive endpoints in a 242-person trial, and the US NTP ran prenatal developmental studies over neural-tube concerns in animals.

Weakly supported claims.

Huperzine A.

  • An alkaloid from Chinese club moss; a genuine acetylcholinesterase inhibitor (donepezil class) active at 200-400 mcg/day in Alzheimer's trials (approved in China, modest dementia signals).

Data are in dementia patients, not healthy adults, and it lingers for hours (~10-14 h half-life), compounding cholinergic load. Real pharmacology.

My position, corrected from my tub labels (October 2026):

  • Never standalone pills — never bought or added — but inside my formulas all along: Igniter Xtreme 2.0 carries DMAE 500 mg + Huperzine-A 200 mcg per V1 dose; Pump Xtreme (Canadian) carries Huperzine-A 50 mcg per V6 dose.

The old absolute-zero claim was wrong; this chapter corrects it. My only cholinergic capsule remains citicoline 250 mg; alpha-GPC only inside ALLMAX formulas.

S016 is not my protocol and appears nowhere in my grids.

Provenance — what the labels actually say

  • The October 2026 label audit changed this story.
  • DMAE and Huperzine-A did not enter my system through any audit layer: they were printed on my own tub panels the whole time.
  • My Igniter Xtreme 2.0 (US tub, 2 scoops / 28 g) lists DMAE bitartrate 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg; my Pump Xtreme (Canadian tub, 2 scoops / 31 g) lists Huperzine-A 50 mcg (from Huperzia serrata).
  • I never bought them as standalone pills and never added them to any grid — which is why the earlier "not in my protocol on any day" claim felt true — but "not as pills" is not the same as "not in my formulas," and the book now says exactly that.
  • The S016 Endurance Pill scrub is a separate matter and it stands: S016 came only from a reconciliation document, never from my shelf or my locked database, and it appears nowhere in my grids.
  • The stale V2.0 website pages still carry the names as standalone items; the books' old error was absolute zero.
  • Correct framing for both: "formula-native only, never standalone pills."

DMAE — chemistry vs claims

  • DMAE (2-dimethylaminoethanol, deanol) is a tertiary amine structurally related to choline.
  • As the bitartrate salt it is sold at 500-2,000 mg/day with claims around focus and acetylcholine support.

The human literature is old, small and inconsistent; a related DMAE compound (DMAE pyroglutamate context in the cited 242-person trial) failed its primary cognitive endpoints.

  • The US National Toxicology Program conducted prenatal developmental toxicity work on DMAE/bitartrate chemistry over neural-tube signal concerns in animals — not proof of human harm at nutrient product doses, but the opposite of a clean, modern evidence base.

For a daily, indefinite stack at 46, "weakly supported and old" describes the evidence; the compound arrived inside a formula taken for other reasons.

Huperzine A — real drug, formula context

  • Huperzine A is not a weak nutrient product claim; it is a potent, reversible acetylcholinesterase inhibitor isolated from Huperzia serrata, used pharmaceutically in China for Alzheimer's disease at 200-400 mcg/day.

Trial signals are modest and concentrated in dementia populations, with gastrointestinal and cholinergic adverse effects consistent with the class (the donepezil class).

  • Its long duration of action means a morning dose would still be inhibiting acetylcholinesterase during my afternoon and night stages, compounding citicoline and formula Alpha-GPC across the entire day — which is exactly why the V1/V6: at most one on training days; neither on rest days firewall is structural, not voluntary.

Why these pill windows differ

Meal-linked: digestive enzymes are placed with the meal they help break down. Fish-oil and botanical softgels stay in the food grid; broad micronutrients serve daily metabolism rather than an acute workout effect.

Training-linked: the separate citicoline primer precedes the selected session. V1 and V6 remain alternative formulas; their native focus ingredients must be counted together with the primer.

Mineral servings: conditional calcium is in the afternoon grid and magnesium glycinate in the night grid. This separates the recorded servings and supports routine and tolerability; calcium does not universally prevent magnesium absorption. Calcium absorption depends on form and elemental dose, with smaller servings generally absorbed more efficiently.

Night-linked: magnesium supports normal nerve, muscle and enzyme function; tart cherry serves the manufacturer’s evening recovery purpose. Night placement is the recorded schedule, not proof that these nutrients only work at night. Optional Medi-C and Hey Relax contribute additional magnesium to the daily total.

Collagen: source, type and purpose

Marine means fish-derived; bovine means cattle-derived. The recorded PROMISE Peptan marine product is type I. Source alone does not establish superiority or identify every type in another product.

Type I provides tensile structure in skin, tendons and bone; type II is associated with cartilage. Hydrolyzed peptides provide amino-acid building blocks for connective-tissue turnover. Undenatured type II is a different formulation and purpose, not the current peptide serving.

Collagen complements muscle-protein foods: its amino-acid pattern is different from whey, casein and the beef-protein matrix.
