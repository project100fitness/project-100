Why the vectors work

One line per vector

Every vector carries one pathway line beside its count, dose and benefit rows. The line explains the system; the rows in Chapter 8 prove it. The two nitric-oxide routes — citrulline in V1/V6, beetroot nitrate in V4 — are kept distinct on purpose.

  • V1 Ignition. Why this works:

    • Citrulline feeds the arginine-nitric-oxide pathway for blood flow
    • Citicoline plus the alpha-GPC in the formula feed acetylcholine for focus and contraction
    • Caffeine blocks adenosine to cut perceived fatigue
    • Beta-alanine builds muscle carnosine to buffer acid as sets get hard
    • Betaine (TMG) donates methyl groups in the methionine cycle while helping cells hold water

    Formula nootropic matrix inside the Igniter base (DMAE 500 mg, Huperzine-A 200 mcg, tyrosine 2,000 mg, choline 400 mg, theanine 400 mg, bacopa 320 mg, cocoa 500 mg, juniper 200 mg — counted, never standalone pills); and a separate cholinergic primer (citicoline 250 mg capsule).

    Creatine is held out of the shaker.

  • V6 Pump. Why this works: nitric-oxide hyperemia without stimulants — citrulline 8,000 mg + added malate, NitroVascine (inositol-stabilized arginine silicate) 250 mg, L-arginine 500 mg (as sulphate), GlycerPump 2,000 mg for hyper-hydration, plus osmolyte symmetry (betaine 5,000 mg on V6 days: 2.5 g native + 2.5 g standalone) and Alpha-GPC inside the base formulas only.

    Zero caffeine; 220 mg formula sodium and 1,000 mg native creatine monohydrate counted in the base.

  • V2 Refuel. Why this works: sodium and potassium maintain fluid balance and nerve-to-muscle signaling during the session, magnesium and calcium support contraction chemistry, and the leucine in the 2:1:1 BCAAs signals the mTOR pathway that switches muscle protein synthesis on.
  • V3 Recovery. Why this works: beef isolate supplies the amino acids muscle rebuilds with; collagen provides glycine and proline, the building blocks of connective tissue; and L-carnitine shuttles fatty acids into the mitochondria, where they can be burned for energy.

  • V4 Reload. Why this works:

    • Beetroot nitrate takes a second, separate route to nitric oxide (nitrate to nitrite to NO) from the citrulline route in V1/V6
    • BioSteel's carbohydrate and protein refill glycogen and start repair
    • Creatine tops up the phosphocreatine store that re-makes ATP between sets
    • R-ALA, taken separately as a whole capsule, supports glucose handling and antioxidant recycling
  • V5 Transport. Why this works: on a rest day the job is maintenance — creatine keeps the phosphocreatine store full for tomorrow, carnitine keeps fatty-acid transport moving, collagen keeps supplying connective-tissue building blocks, and the RapiDrem electrolytes keep fluid balance steady without a training stimulus.

  • V10 Shutdown. Why this works:

    • Glycine is an inhibitory neurotransmitter and a collagen building block, associated with calmer, cooler sleep
    • Glutamine is a preferred fuel for gut and immune cells
    • Creatine quietly maintains the phosphocreatine store overnight
    • The optional Medi-C Plus vitamin C is the cofactor the body needs to turn collagen amino acids into actual collagen

Lens 1:

  • One cholinergic capsule (citicoline 250 mg) plus formula-native cholinergics inside the pre-workout bases (Ch. 22)
  • Creatine is held out of the V1 shaker so the pump chemistry stays auditable
  • V6 carries 1.0 g native monohydrate counted in the base

Lens 2:

  • Alpha-GPC is counted only where it actually enters — inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day 500 mg)
  • DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg are counted where they actually enter — inside the V1 Igniter formula
  • Huperzine-A 50 mcg inside the V6 Pump formula

Lens 5: Cholinergic load is capped by architecture (V1 xor V6; citicoline sole capsule; the two formula Huperzine-A doses never stack same-day, half-life ~10-24 h) rather than by willpower — the firewall is the safety mechanism.

Why the vectors work

Five lenses on the pathways

Lens 1 — Physiology. The pathway map is deliberately non-redundant: one cholinergic capsule (citicoline 250 mg) plus formula-native cholinergics inside the pre-workout bases (Ch. 22), one adrenergic vector (V1), one stimulant-free NO vector (V6), one methylation/osmolyte anchor (betaine, both ignition modes), and night inhibitory chemistry (magnesium glycinate, glycine, tart cherry).

Each mechanism has a single primary owner so dose accounting (Ch. 12) stays arithmetic, not guesswork.

Lens 2 — Measurement. Pathway claims are tied to countable payloads:

  • Citrulline totals are stated as free-base equivalents (10.7-12 g), betaine is brand-and dose-locked (NOW Sports, 2,500 mg), and Alpha-GPC is counted only where it actually enters — inside ALLMAX formulas (V6 day 500 mg)
  • DMAE 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg are counted where they actually enter — inside the V1 Igniter formula
  • Huperzine-A 50 mcg inside the V6 Pump formula

A mechanism without a counted dose does not appear in this chapter.

Lens 3 — Training. Timing is mechanism:

  • Citicoline at 05:30/T-30 precedes the session it supports
  • R-ALA precedes the V4 shake and is read against its 19 g carbohydrate context
  • The 10:30 band deload sits between session and reload as blood-flow volume that does not become a second workout

Pathways are scheduled, not just listed.

Lens 4 — Nutrition & digestion. The post-cholecystectomy gut shapes pathway choices: taurine's bile-conjugation role, meal enzymes with every solid meal, chewed dates instead of blended intra-workout carbohydrate, and conditional liver items (Liver-GI, milk thistle) that support conjugation without pretending to replace a gallbladder.

Lens 5 — Laboratory & safety. Piperine (5 mg) is the chapter's honest double-edge: it raises curcumin exposure by slowing clearance, and that same clearance-slowing is why it is watched (Ch. 13).

Cholinergic load is capped by architecture (V1 xor V6; citicoline sole capsule; the two formula Huperzine-A doses never stack same-day, half-life ~10-24 h) rather than by willpower — the firewall is the safety mechanism.

Two compounds inside my formulas

My position

DMAE (dimethylaminoethanol). A choline analog (DMAE bitartrate, typically 500-2,000 mg/day). Human evidence is thin and old; a related compound failed cognitive endpoints in a 242-person trial, and the US NTP ran prenatal developmental studies over neural-tube concerns in animals.

Weakly supported claims.

Huperzine A. An alkaloid from Chinese club moss; a genuine acetylcholinesterase inhibitor (donepezil class) active at 200-400 mcg/day in Alzheimer's trials (approved in China, modest dementia signals).

Data are in dementia patients, not healthy adults, and it lingers for hours (~10-14 h half-life), compounding cholinergic load. Real pharmacology.

My position, corrected from my tub labels (October 2026): Never standalone pills — never bought or added — but inside my formulas all along: Igniter Xtreme 2.0 carries DMAE 500 mg + Huperzine-A 200 mcg per V1 dose; Pump Xtreme (Canadian) carries Huperzine-A 50 mcg per V6 dose.

The old absolute-zero claim was wrong; this chapter corrects it. My only cholinergic capsule remains citicoline 250 mg; alpha-GPC only inside ALLMAX formulas.

S016 is not my protocol and appears nowhere in my grids.

Two compounds inside my formulas

What the labels actually say

How these two names entered my documents, what the evidence actually says, and how the labels corrected the record. I keep this investigation in the master so the correction is reasoned, not just asserted.

Provenance — what the labels actually say

The October 2026 label audit changed this story. DMAE and Huperzine-A did not enter my system through any audit layer: they were printed on my own tub panels the whole time.

My Igniter Xtreme 2.0 (US tub, 2 scoops / 28 g) lists DMAE bitartrate 500 mg and Huperzine-A 200 mcg; my Pump Xtreme (Canadian tub, 2 scoops / 31 g) lists Huperzine-A 50 mcg (from Huperzia serrata).

I never bought them as standalone pills and never added them to any grid — which is why the earlier "not in my protocol on any day" claim felt true — but "not as pills" is not the same as "not in my formulas," and the book now says exactly that.

The S016 Endurance Pill scrub is a separate matter and it stands: S016 came only from a reconciliation document, never from my shelf or my locked database, and it appears nowhere in my grids.

The stale V2.0 website pages still carry the names as standalone items; the books' old error was absolute zero. Correct framing for both: "formula-native only, never standalone pills."

DMAE — chemistry vs claims

DMAE (2-dimethylaminoethanol, deanol) is a tertiary amine structurally related to choline. As the bitartrate salt it is sold at 500-2,000 mg/day with claims around focus and acetylcholine support.

The human literature is old, small and inconsistent; a related DMAE compound (DMAE pyroglutamate context in the cited 242-person trial) failed its primary cognitive endpoints.

The US National Toxicology Program conducted prenatal developmental toxicity work on DMAE/bitartrate chemistry over neural-tube signal concerns in animals — not proof of human harm at supplement doses, but the opposite of a clean, modern evidence base.

For a daily, indefinite stack at 46, "weakly supported and old" describes the evidence; the compound arrived inside a formula taken for other reasons.

Huperzine A — real drug, formula context

Huperzine A is not a weak supplement claim; it is a potent, reversible acetylcholinesterase inhibitor isolated from Huperzia serrata, used pharmaceutically in China for Alzheimer's disease at 200-400 mcg/day.

Trial signals are modest and concentrated in dementia populations, with gastrointestinal and cholinergic adverse effects consistent with the class (the donepezil class).

Its long duration of action means a morning dose would still be inhibiting acetylcholinesterase during my afternoon and night stages, compounding citicoline and formula Alpha-GPC across the entire day — which is exactly why the V1 xor V6 firewall is structural, not voluntary.

Physiology: my acetylcholine support is precursors plus a formula-native acetylcholinesterase inhibitor — the inhibitor does not substitute for the precursors, it compounds them, which is why the V1 xor V6 firewall (never the same day) is the load control.

Evidence: DMAE's human support is old and weak; Huperzine-A's support is dementia-specific — neither was ever a deliberate addition, both arrived inside formulas I take for other reasons.

My position stands: formula-native only, never standalone pills.

  • State C evening architecture: no V1, no morning citicoline — the single 250 mg capsule travels to T-30 before V6; V6 provides pump chemistry without caffeine, V2 fuels, V4 reloads, night stage/V10 close sodium-free.
  • Rest day (State B) as active design: wake pack, food, meal enzymes, V5 transport and V10 close stay; stimulation and training grids leave.
  • Post-cholecystectomy operating system: smaller fat portions (69 g/day lipid ceiling distributed), Pure Encapsulations Digestive Enzymes Ultra (1,500 FIP lipase) with every solid meal in a 3x rotation, taurine bile conjugation, chewed carbohydrate, conditional liver support against a periodic hepatic panel.
  • FFMI and the drug-free boundary: FFMI 25.71 at FFM 162-164 lb already sits at the 25.0-25.5 elite natural boundary; 100 lb true would need FFMI ~27.2 — why 100 stays the disclosed stretch and 90-93 stays the scoreboard.
  • Good scan day: wake, void, scan fasted on the same InBody at Gym Fit Forme, log as S with SMM, weight and body fat from one visit, then let the December 2026 review window — not the drive home — produce the decision.
  • Asset index: vector infographics V1-V6 and V10 (white/dark, my likeness), anatomical progress graphic, supplement station, pill organizer, gym photographs and hex-grid station — every caption repeats its grid's count, dose and use and names its governing chapter.
