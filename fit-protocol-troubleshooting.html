11Check Engine Light

The Failure Analysis Matrix

Symptoms are diagnostic data, not obstacles. Identify the code, execute the override.

ERR_DIGEST_01
Cement Gut
Fiber overload or missing enzyme. Fix: cease solid food 24h, deploy Fusion 3A.
ERR_LIPID_01
Hard Gut Sludge
Fat-cap breach past 69g. Fix: solvent flush — hot ACV tea + 24h zero-fat isolate.
ERR_ULNAR_01
Elbow Click
Grip too tight / supination breach. Fix: HXD-ERGO/Angles90 grips + silent eccentrics.
ERR_L4S1_01
Back Stiffness
Compression / disc guarding reflex. Fix: 60-second dead hang + more magnesium at 20:30.
ERR_RENAL_01
Frothy Urine
Protein-ceiling breach. Fix: drop to 200g for 48h, rebuild in 10g/day increments.
ERR_HYPO_01
PM Dizziness
Hypoglycemia. Fix: emergency shuttle — 0.5 scoop BioSteel or 1 banana immediately.
ERR_BILE_01
Morning Sludge
Overnight bile accumulation. Fix: +25% Metamucil/greens dose at 06:00.
ERR_METAB_01
Stalled Fat Loss
Hidden "ghost sugar" calories. Fix: weekend audit — purge honey, sauces, breading.
Diagnostic, Not Defeat

Every symptom has a documented cause and a fix — the check-engine light is information, not failure.
