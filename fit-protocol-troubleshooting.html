7 · The Failure Matrix and Correction Method

Safety & Clinical

Diagnose before escalating: measurement, sleep, digestion and load are checked before any dose changes.

Protocol reference · PDF page 11
Symptom codeFirst checksCorrection before escalation
Stalled SMM estimateSame-device conditions? Hydration, glycogen, recent training?Repeat standardized BIA over 2-3 weeks; review performance trend.
Poor sleepCaffeine total and timing, TeaCrine half-life (16-20 h), awakeningsPull caffeine earlier; do not add sedative stacks reflexively.
Loose stoolsMagnesium total, vitamin C dose, fat bolus size, bile patternSplit fat, review osmotic stack with clinician.
Joint / nerve pain flareNew exercise, range or load jump?Return to last tolerated setup; assess if progressive.
Afternoon energy crashSleep debt, post-workout carbohydrate (V4 only 19 g), ALA timingAddress sleep and fuel before stimulants.

Suggested reductions are not adopted changes until the user confirms them. A removed item cannot reappear because an old page includes it.

Daily guide notes · Symptom Codes and the Failure Matrix

Symptom Codes and the Failure Matrix

Daily schedule · PDF page 12
PatternMost likely stackFirst response
Headache + vivid dreams + low moodCholinergic vectorsRemove newest cholinergic; 72-hour log
Urgent GI after mealsMg + Vit C + fat bolusSeparate in time; spread fat; recheck scenario total
Sweats / shakes without causeALA / glucose vectorsStop vector; seek clinical advice promptly
Poor sleep after training daysCaffeine timingMove cut-off earlier; do not add sleep aids into the stack blindly
RPE drift, HRV suppressedTotal loadDeload; audit sleep and intake before nutrient products

Failure rule: ambition never overrides a stop rule. A paused protocol is the system working, not failing.
