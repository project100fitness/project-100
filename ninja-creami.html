Liquids + protein

    Texture calibration

    Target before freezing: 600–645 g [21.2–22.8 oz] per pint in the book. The tub’s MAX FILL line always takes priority. Do not force a batch to meet both by overfilling.

    Prep / freeze / process

    Seven steps. A repeatable pint.

    Book quantities with the NC500-series manufacturer’s operating baseline. The illustrated V2.14 book remains unchanged. The new books describe an eight-step owner record; the lid-off first freeze is explicitly an owner deviation from the boxed-manual lid-on instruction. Current web operation and practice differences are explained below.

    1. 01 / Weigh dry ingredients

      Choose the powder, gum dose and flavour card. Dry-mix the gum through the other powders before hydration to reduce clumps. A spoon is a convenience measure; the calibrated gram weight governs.

    2. 02 / Hydrate + blend

      Hydrate the broth and collagen in the hot water in a separate heatproof vessel. Add cold milk and pasteurized liquid egg whites, then the dry matrix. Blend until uniform; the book records about 90 seconds.

    3. 03 / Settle + check

      Rest the mixed base for about 15 minutes. Weigh the batch against the book’s 600–645 g [21.2–22.8 oz] range and check the tub’s MAX FILL line.

    4. 04 / Freeze level

      Fit the lid. Freeze on a level surface for at least 24 hours. The manufacturer gives a freezer range of −13 to −22°C [9 to −7°F].

    5. 05 / Inspect the surface

      If frozen unevenly, melt in the refrigerator, whisk and refreeze level. Do not drill or forcefully break a frozen block with a knife.

    6. 06 / Process + finish

      Use LITE ICE CREAM for low-fat or sugar-substitute bases. If adding inclusions, use the MIX-IN route before any RE-SPIN. Without inclusions, RE-SPIN can finish a powdery result. The book’s optional 1–2 tbsp [15–30 mL / 0.5–1 fl oz] milk is owner texture tuning.

    7. 07 / Clean + store

      Unplug. Clean removable parts separately; wipe the motor base and spindle with a damp cloth. Smooth leftovers with a spoon before refreezing. Keep ingredients refrigerated until preparation.

    Three inclusion tiers · keep crunch and swirls distinct

    01 / Machine mix-in

    Small chopped nuts, cacao nibs or mini chips. Make the manual’s spoon cavity in the already processed portion, then MIX-IN. It is not a hole drilled into solid frozen base.

    02 / Fold gently

    Fragile protein dough and crunch bits: fold with a spoon after processing. Where the edition gives no weight, the web guide does not invent one.

    03 / Ribbon last

    Caramel or fudge: fold lightly through the finished cream for a visible swirl. Added sauces and inclusions change nutrition totals.

    Food science · what the numbers do and do not establish

    Sweetener and freezing

    The book’s 1.9× allulose figure comes from sucrose-to-allulose molecular masses, about 342.3 ÷ 180.16. That ideal particle-count comparison is not a measured freezing curve for this milk/protein mixture. Sweetener blends with different bulking agents will behave differently.

    Gum concentration

    In a 600 g base, 0.5 g is 0.083% by weight; 1.0 g is 0.167%. The edition’s “0.25% anti-slime law” is a kitchen texture guideline, not a universal cross-link threshold. Powder, starch, gums and processing all matter.

    Protein is not all a stabilizer

    Hydrolyzed collagen peptides are not gelatin. Broth and collagen are recorded here as protein ingredients; they do not prove a gel network. Cream of tartar at the cold recipe dose is a flavour/acidity adjustment.

    800 W is a model context

    The book’s “lathe” description is an analogy for processing a frozen base. It is not a measured force, temperature or motor-load model. Confirm the model and rating on your own appliance label.

    Book practice versus the machine manual
    Covered freezingThe edition labels initial lid-off freezing as owner practice. This web workflow uses the manual’s lid-on, level, at-least-24-hour baseline.
    Surface preparationOlder plates show crushing peaks and a knife borehole. Use the manual’s melt/whisk/refreeze remedy for uneven freezing; the mix-in cavity is made with a spoon after processing.
    Program choiceThe book sometimes groups Sorbet with lean protein bases. The manual describes SORBET for fruit-based, high-water/sugar mixtures. LITE ICE CREAM is the lean/substitute-sugar choice here.
    Finish orderThe manual says not to RE-SPIN before MIX-IN. The book’s dry re-spin advice applies here only when following the no-inclusion route.
    CleanupWarm-water rinsing applies to removable parts. The motor housing and spindle are wiped with a damp cloth, not flushed.
    AuthorityRecipe weights remain the supplied edition’s record. Model-specific manufacturer limits take priority over archived diagrams or general kitchen descriptions.

    Troubleshooting · diagnose the base before adding more

    Gummy or slimy

    Check the scale, powder family and pudding setting first. Do not stack the full isolate gum dose with a starch-bearing blend or pudding. The book’s 1.0 g / 0.5 g settings are tested kitchen calibrations, not an interchangeable volume standard for every gum.

    Powdery after processing

    Check freezing conditions and the recipe composition. On the no-inclusion route, try the manual’s RE-SPIN option; the book adds milk only when needed. Adding liquid repeatedly changes the base and macro ledger.

    Hard bits or lost crunch

    Do not process loose hard frozen ingredients on their own. Keep machine additions small and chopped; protect delicate bits by hand-folding. Put a ribbon in last, rather than blending it back into the base.

    Numbers do not reconcile

    Check the milk ledger, actual scoop mass and all additions. The book’s recorded energy and macro estimates are preserved without claiming a new label audit. See its reconciliation register before comparing editions.

    Operator context · the edition’s personal record

    The book records a 200.1 lb operator context and historical planning figures of 2,780 kcal / 252 g protein / 69 g fat. These describe that edition’s framework. They are not measured whole-day intake, new site-wide targets or a prescription for another reader.
