The Master Workflow

Prep → Emulsify → Settle → Freeze → Level → Spin → Flush

0.1g digital-scale accuracy on the dry matrix. A hot/cold thermal-phase vortex blend. A 15-minute settle. Lid-off freeze for 2–4h, then lid-on for 16–24h at -18°C to -20°C. A Deiss masher plus warm-water bath to level the surface. A Lite Ice Cream / Sorbet spin with re-spin logic. Hot-water spindle flush after every single use — no exceptions.

Hardware & Safety

Don't Skip These

Critical Hardware Limits

-18°C (0°F) is the safe freeze floor. Below -20°C (-4°F), risk of an unspinnable "cement brick" rises sharply and strains the motor — don't chase a colder freezer setting thinking it helps. Never exceed the Max Fill line. The surface must be 100% level before spinning. Flush the spindle with hot water immediately after every single use.

Re-Spin Logic

If the first spin leaves the base under-processed, a short dry re-spin (no added liquid) almost always finishes the job — adding liquid before a re-spin is one of the most common mistakes in the log below.

If It Freezes Uneven

Crush The Peaks

Press the Deiss masher straight down, firm and even — never twist. Crush any high spots before the surface pass.

If The Spin Stalls

Drill A Borehole

A clean knife, straight down to the bottom, gives the paddle a center channel to bite into on a hard-frozen pint.

Before You Refreeze

Re-Flatten The Top

Level any leftover ice cream back down with the spoon before it goes back in the freezer for next time.
