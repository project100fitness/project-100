The Master Workflow

Prep → Emulsify → Settle → Freeze → Level → Spin → Flush

0.1g digital-scale accuracy on the dry matrix. A hot/cold thermal-phase vortex blend. A 15-minute settle. Lid-off freeze for 2–4h, then lid-on for 16–24h at -18°C to -20°C. A Deiss masher plus warm-water bath to level the surface. A Lite Ice Cream / Sorbet spin with re-spin logic. Hot-water spindle flush after every single use — no exceptions.

Hardware & Safety

Don't Skip These

Critical Hardware Limits

Never process below -22°C unthawed — it produces an unspinnable "cement brick" that can strain the motor. Never exceed the Max Fill line. The surface must be 100% level before spinning. Flush the spindle with hot water immediately after every single use.

Re-Spin Logic

If the first spin leaves the base under-processed, a short dry re-spin (no added liquid) almost always finishes the job — adding liquid before a re-spin is one of the most common mistakes in the log below.
