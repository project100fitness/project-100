Recovery & Logistics

Equipment manifest →

A three-tier foam roller set, and the duffel and organizer that carry the kit.

The actual rig, band-by-band and handle-by-handle — straight from the PROJ3K_100 equipment manifest, not stock photos.

Recovery tools

  • jooeer 3-in-1 Nested Foam Roller Set

    3 NESTED TIERS

    Purpose: Nested rollers and a massage stick offer different contact surfaces.

    Usage: Choose the tool and pressure for a comfortable rolling session.

    Design & specifications

    Outer: 18 × 5.5 in black high-density foam roller with a pyramid/spiked texture. Middle: 18 × 3.9 in neon-orange roller with linear ridges.

    Core: 18 × 1.57 in orange segmented massage stick with matte-gray grips and a black lanyard.

Gear storage & logistics

  • MIAOKE Tactical Hardware Duffel

    Duffel + organizer

    Purpose: A duffel and organizer keep attachments together for transport.

    Usage: Separate hardware and small items into the recorded storage compartments.

    Design & specifications

    Black utility duffel/backpack with red zipper tracks and red diamond elastic side cording. The matching clear-panel organizer is labeled WASHBAG.

Equipment identities and listed ratings follow the supplied PROJECT 100 visual recognition database. Tube ratings, combined set ratings and force at a particular stretch are different measurements.
