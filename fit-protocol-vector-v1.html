⚡ V1 IGNITION — Key Actives

  • 8gCitrulline
  • 3.2gBeta-Alanine
  • 373mgCaffeine
  • 500mgDMAE
  • 200mcgHuperzine-A
  • 2.5gBetaine
  • Citrulline (8g): Nitric oxide precursor for blood flow and muscle pump. This dose is at the top of the studied range (6-8g), supporting vascularity and nutrient delivery during training.
  • Beta-Alanine (3.2g): Full daily dose for muscular endurance. Buffers acid buildup in muscles, allowing more reps before fatigue. The tingling sensation is harmless and expected.
  • Caffeine (373mg): Central nervous system stimulant for focus and energy. This is a high dose — approaching the 400mg daily guidance limit. Includes tea timing considerations.
  • DMAE (500mg) + Huperzine-A (200mcg): Cholinergic stack for focus and mind-muscle connection. DMAE supports choline levels; Huperzine-A extends acetylcholine activity. Formula-native from the Igniter tub — never standalone pills.
  • Betaine (2.5g): Supports power output and cellular hydration. The most-studied dose for performance benefits.

All compounds are formula-native from Bogdan's labeled tubs — never standalone pills. See label audit 6 Oct 2026 for ground truth.

Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
About 373 mg total caffeine, 10.67-12 g active citrulline base, 3.2 g beta-alanine, 2 g PeakO2 mushrooms, 2.5 g betaine. Nootropic layer is formula-native only, never standalone pills: DMAE bitartrate 500 mg + huperzine-A 200 mcg inside the Igniter. Spaced about 2 hours post- breakfast tea.
Stack interaction
IGNITER about 373 mg caffeine + TeaCrine (theacrine half-life 16-20 h) + Dynamine + theobromine + Earl Grey 40-70 mg — Exposure regularly crosses the 400 mg/day guidance; sleep is the limiting organ.
Margin
IGNITER about 373 mg caffeine before tea; with tea about 413–443 mg versus Health Canada 400 mg/day guidance.
Monitor
Sleep duration and awakenings; session RPE and performance.
Stop rule
Palpitations, fainting or sleep disruption that does not resolve when caffeine is pulled earlier.
