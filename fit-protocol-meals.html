Folder 04

Digestion and chemistry

Without a gallbladder, fat handling is a rate problem: smaller portions, slower eating and meal enzymes with every solid meal.

Nutrition and digestion, five lenses

Lens 1 — Physiology. Without a gallbladder, bile delivery is continuous but dilute; fat handling becomes a rate problem. My ceilings encode that: 69 g lipids/day distributed across smaller portions, Pure Encapsulations Digestive Enzymes Ultra (1,500 FIP lipase) with every solid meal — the every-stack enzyme in a 3x rotation (Ch. 11), and taurine's bile-acid conjugation role in the vectors.

Historical steatosis is why liver items are conditional and hepatic panels are periodic.

Lens 2 — Measurement. Whole-day totals (2,780 kcal, 252 g protein, 4.0 L hydration) are only honest when the whole day was measured; legacy meals (MS-01 Mega Shake, SB-01 Salad Bowl) stay in rotation precisely because their labels were checked and recorded.

Lens 3 — Training. Protein is distributed so each meal clears the leucine threshold at 46 rather than dumped once; carbohydrate is placed where glycogen is spent (breakfast base, V2 dates chewed separately, V4 19 g reload) and never blended into a form that competes with amino delivery.

Lens 4 — Digestion. Chewed vs blended is a formulation decision in this system: dates and the MegaFood C gummy are chewed; greens sit in their own glass; R-ALA is swallowed whole. Gastric handling is treated as chemistry, not convenience.

Lens 5 — Laboratory. Afternoon testosterone draws are non-diagnostic by timing (my 13:28, 7.4 nmol/L example); morning fasting draws carry verdict weight.

Biotin 425 mcg is disclosed for assay-specific reading. What I eat and when I draw blood are one integrity system.
