Machine Corner

High-Tensile Resistance

KUZARO Pure Triad Tubing Set35–85 LBS

The core band system — six tubes on a strict color-to-tension gradient, light gray at 35 lbs up to deep solid black at the 85 lb top end.

HPYGN Heavy-Duty Compression Set330 LBS

Twin multi-layered resistance lines. Doubled and shortened at the rig's lowest anchor point, it runs a constant 130 lb baseline tension.

Tribe Lifting Modular Resistance BarChrome

Knurled 3-piece threaded bar with independent 360° swivel rings on each end — tangle-free band loading.

HPYGN Heavy Pilates BarPadded

Foam-padded steel core bar with red-stitched nylon terminal straps for band-resisted presses and rows.

Grip & Handle Accessories

Angles90 (A90) Pull-Up GripsRotating

Bright orange crescent-shaped handles on a nylon sling — lets the wrist rotate naturally through a pull instead of locking it flat.

MANUEKLEAR Neoprene Multi-Grip Handle3-Zone

Wide neoprene strap with three stitched grip zones (upper/mid/lower) for fast range-of-motion changes mid-set, single D-ring anchor.

HXD-ERGO Biomechanical HandlesCurved

Asymmetrical curved grip molds built into a fixed-carabiner nylon strap — matches the natural hand angle under load.

INNSTAR Stirrup HandlesHoneycomb

Classic stirrup layout wrapped in an anti-slip silicone honeycomb texture for wet-hand grip security.

THEFITGUY Single-Arm Tricep Rope16.5"

Isolated single-arm rope with a bright red bell-shaped rubber stop — the handle visible in the rig photo above.

BLUSLM Ergonomic Tricep RopeDual-Arm

Extra-long braided rope terminating in molded rubber palm-flare shields — no round-ball slippage.

Harnesses, Straps & Anchors

THEFITGUY Sled Pulling Belt & Straps9.8 ft ×2

Padded neoprene waist harness with two heavy-duty D-rings and matching pulling straps for sled/band drags.

THEFITGUY Ultimate Extension StrapsDual

Flat nylon extension straps with steel D-ring terminals — extends band anchor points around the rig.

VEHICLEX Axle Tie-Down StrapsIndustrial

Car-hauling-grade webbing repurposed as a heavy anchor strap, triangular steel D-rings at both ends.

NILIGHT Soft Loop Anchor Straps1.7"×17"

Short choke-loop straps rigged directly around structural gym bars for a quick, non-marking anchor point.

Oak-Sports / JRSGS Carabiners25kN

The yellow- and red-gated locking clips visible throughout the rig — rated to 5,600 lbs, one on nearly every band end.

Recovery & Logistics

jooeer 3-in-1 Nested Foam Roller18"

Three nested tiers — a spiked trigger-point outer roller, a ribbed neon-orange mid roller, and a core massage stick.

Neoprene Bundle Management Sleeves1.5"

Hook-and-loop cylindrical wraps that keep multi-band arrays from tangling or friction-wearing against each other.

MIAOKE Tactical Hardware DuffelGear Bag

Ruggedized utility duffel with a separate clear-panel travel organizer for the small stuff — clips, chalk, bands.
