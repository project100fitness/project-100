Questions

Frequently Asked.

What is PROJECT 100?
A documented personal comeback at 46: the goal is 100 lb of bioimpedance-estimated skeletal muscle below 15% body fat, with no guaranteed timeline.
Is this a bulk or a cut?

Neither, run separately — both, run at once. The goal is stripping stored body fat to reveal the structure underneath while forcing skeletal muscle mass upward at the same time: the narrow path where mass meets definition.

Where can I follow the day-to-day training?

The Fit Workout page is the full training log — every block, filterable by category, with real dates and engagement, updated as sessions happen.

What's the Fit Protocol?
FIT PROTOCOL is the system behind the lived record: focused files for daily execution, vectors, nutrition, training and monitoring.
How is the nutrition handled?

The Fit Nutrition page has the real recipes, meals, and the macro protocol that fuels the excavation — nothing theoretical, all logged.

Where do new updates post first?

New training blocks, recovery meals, and protocol updates post to @fudge_fit on Instagram as they happen — the site catches up from there.
