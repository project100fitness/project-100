Clinical notes · five lenses
Mechanism
Protein, adequate energy and meal tolerance support repeatable training.
Stack interaction
Fat boluses, supplemental magnesium and vitamin C compound GI tolerance in the recorded no-gallbladder context.
Margin
Known totals are partial labels. Training supplemental Mg baseline 341 mg; options may increase it to 646 mg versus 350 mg/day supplemental UL.
Monitor
Record stool, abdominal pain and the optional items actually taken.
Stop rule
Recurrent watery stools with weight loss, oily stools or significant abdominal pain require assessment.
