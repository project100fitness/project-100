Protein Fudge & Treats

Chocolate Cacao & Peanut Rice-Cake Sandwiches

Fudge spread variation · owner confirmed; amounts extrapolated

Explore recipe & sources →
Ingredients & preparation
  • Rice cakes — use two for each closed sandwich
  • Chocolate, cacao and peanut powder mixture — confirmed by the owner
  • Chocolate protein powder — inferred from the recurring fudge baseline
  • Almond milk or protein milk — recurring liquid options confirmed by the owner; add gradually for a spreadable texture
  • Optional collagen, pinch of xanthan gum and salt from the related fudge recipe; not confirmed in this photographed batch
  1. Mix the chocolate protein, cacao and peanut powder. The jar recipe offers a working starting ratio of 2 scoops protein to 1/2 cup peanut powder; amounts for this batch are unmeasured.
  2. Add milk a little at a time until the mixture holds as a thick spread. Use less liquid than a drinkable smoothie.
  3. Spread over one rice cake and top with another; repeat for the quantity you want. Serve soon to keep the rice cakes crisp.
